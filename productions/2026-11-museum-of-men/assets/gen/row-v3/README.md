## How the picks were chosen

I checked the full frames plus zoomed crops of each plinth.

- **9:16:** in round 1, c1, c2 and c4 all fixed the box (yellowed edges, moulded tray, no dot) and the drawer density. c2's and c4's T-shirt prints had letter-like shapes (c4 read like a word), so they were rejected. **c1** has a cracked brownish figure print with no lettering, ~9 keys including the black-headed one and the long iron key, a dense black and white drawer, and even the chappal safety pin. The 4:5 and 1:1 round-1 candidates either kept the v2 sleek box (copied from their v2 scene ref) or had the phone too high, so those aspects were redone from the 9:16 pick.
- **4:5:** in round 2, **c3** places the phone lowest (phone top at ≈29% of the height) and keeps the drawer fully in frame. c2 cut the drawer at the bottom. c1 and c4 had the phone higher.
- **1:1:** in round 2, **c2** has the phone top at ≈28%, with the drawer fully inside the frame. c3 sat higher (≈25%), c1 was a side-angle view, and c4 had a lighter, different set. Round 3 asked for the camera to pull back further, but every candidate came back *higher*, so none was used.
