# Generated images: The Museum of Men (provenance record)

- **Model:** `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana Pro / Gemini 3 Pro Image), through the Gemini API `generateContent`. The models list offered both `gemini-3-pro-image` (GA) and `gemini-3-pro-image-preview`. I used the GA id. No fallback to `gemini-2.5-flash-image` was needed.
- **Config:** `responseModalities: [IMAGE]`, `imageConfig: {aspectRatio: "4:5", imageSize: "2K"}`. Output was 1856×2304 px (≈4:5), delivered by the API as JPEG.
- **Date:** 2026-11-18. Generated by Claude (Showrunner session) in autonomous mode for this one task. The spec is `../../image-prompts.md`.
- **Files:** `slide-0N-cK.jpg` holds every candidate, re-encoded as JPEG q88 to keep the repo small. They are JPG, not PNG, because the API returns JPEG. `pick/slide-0N.png` is the chosen image per slide at full resolution, PNG as requested (a lossless wrapper of the JPEG source). `contact-sheet.jpg` shows all 9 picks in order.
- **Review:** I looked at every candidate myself, as a triptych and with zoomed crops of the object. I rejected any with readable text, logos, people or hands, a wrong-looking object, a missing white plinth, too little empty wall at the top, or heavy artefacts. Up to 4 regeneration rounds were allowed. The most any slide used was 3 rounds (9 images).
- **Disclosure:** every image here is fully AI-generated. No real photograph and no third-party image was used as input.

## Weak spots and open items (honest list)

- **Set consistency:** all 9 share the warm-white wall, pale concrete floor, white plinth and soft top light. The walls on slides 4 and 8 are slightly cooler and greyer than the rest, and slide 9 is deliberately dimmer and warmer ("lights dimmed softly"). A light grade match in Lightroom would even these out.
- **Framing varies a bit:** the plinth sizes and camera height change from slide to slide (3 is low and wide, 7 is tall and narrow). Every pick keeps at least about 35% clean wall at the top. Most keep about 45-55%.
- **Slide 4:** tiny illegible stamped marks on two key bows (see above).
- **Slide 7:** the cover reads as a heat-sealed sleeve more than a moulded fitted cover.
- **Slide 9:** a small scribble mark on the distant T-shirt, and a possible duplicate remote at the far end.
- **Slide 8:** the screen is plain grey, but the perspective of the screen is not perfectly square-on. The code composite needs a slight corner-pin, not a flat paste.
- None of the slides failed outright. Slides 3, 4 and 9 needed the most work.
