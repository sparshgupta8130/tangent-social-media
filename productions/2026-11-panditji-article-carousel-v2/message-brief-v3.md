# Message Brief v3: Panditji article carousel (after the second reader feedback)

v2 failed a first-time reader, a senior communications professional, on three counts:
- the cover was too verbose;
- the slides didn't read as one story, so it wasn't clear what the post was about;
- there was too much text on every slide.

Root causes: the post tried to be four things at once (an app pitch, a build story, an AI
lesson and an article plug); the "one-line message" chained three ideas; and every slide's
second line added a new idea. v3 follows her process: one message, then a 3-5 beat arc, then a
story check on the arc alone, then content. Nothing is designed until Sparsh approves this file.

**What kind of post:** a short story about one decision in building the app. The article is
the depth, offered on the end slide. The app is mentioned, not pitched.

**One-line message:** My astrology app doesn't let AI do the maths.

**Audience:** A (everyday viewer). B gets the depth from the caption and the article.
**Why they'd care:** "Oh, AI can give you two different answers? Good that this one checks."

**Tone:** person-direct.

## The arc (the story check: read only these lines, top to bottom)

| Slide | Beat (the only text on the slide) | Words | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover | Why my astrology app doesn't let AI do the maths | 9 | The app's answer screen on a phone |
| 2 | I asked an AI the same astrology question twice. | 8 | One question, typed twice |
| 3 | It put Saturn in two different signs. | 7 | Two answers side by side: Pisces ≠ Aries |
| 4 | AI guesses. It doesn't calculate. | 5 | A chatbot with a question mark |
| 5 | So my app does the maths, and AI just explains it. | 11 | Calculator → AI explains |
| 6 | Now its answers match NASA's planet data. | 7 | NASA vs Panditji, with a tick |
| 7 | (End slide) Read the full story on Medium. Link in bio. | | Medium card + "Try it free" card |
| 8 | (End slide) Follow along for more things I build with AI. | | Channels |

The story: someone (I) hits a problem (two answers), learns the cause (AI guesses), makes a
change (the app does the maths) and gets a result (it matches NASA). Each beat follows from
the last.

What moved off the slides, and where it went:
- Western vs Indian astrology mixing, birth-time maths and source badges → the article.
- The 427 safety tests → the caption.

Nothing is lost; it moved to where interested people look.

## Caption (draft)
Why my astrology app doesn't let AI do the maths.

I asked an AI the same astrology question twice and got Saturn in two different signs. So in Panditji, the app works out where the planets are, and the AI only explains them.

I also tested it with 427 tricky questions in English, Hindi and Hinglish. The full story is on Medium. Link in bio.

Made with help from Claude. #Panditji #Astrology #AI

## Facts (all from the article)
- Asked about career timing twice, the model put Saturn in one sign and then the
  neighbouring sign. ("astrology question" is the plain form.)
- "Answering from memory causes a model to ... guess" → "AI guesses. It doesn't calculate."
- Deterministic code on the phone computes; the model only explains → beat 5.
- Planet longitudes match NASA JPL / Swiss Ephemeris to within 1 arcsecond → beat 6.
