# Cold read, round 1 (phase adhoc, on the copy): verdict

One-line message: "I built an astrology app where the AI isn't allowed to guess where the planets are."
Audience: A+B, so both personas must pass.

## Meaning (a)
- A RESTATED: "Some guy built a free astrology app where a normal program calculates the planet
  positions so the AI can't get them wrong, and the AI just explains them." It matches: same
  subject, same main claim.
- B RESTATED: "A guy built a free astrology app where regular code calculates the planet
  positions (checked against NASA) and the AI only explains them, because the AI alone kept
  getting positions wrong." It matches.

## Persona A: FAIL
- (b) The cover was read correctly, but "would stop" was only "maybe, leaning yes".
- (c) FAIL. Two confusions on the cover ("Panditji", "isn't allowed to guess": guess what?).
  Ten in total, including the slide 3 headline ("answered from memory").
- (d) FAIL. FELT LIKE was "a presentation or lecture", triggered by slide 3's "Western and Vedic
  astrology count the signs about 24 degrees apart." A also never learned where to get the
  app: "I wanted to know where to get the app."

## Persona B: FAIL
- (b) PASS: would stop.
- (c) FAIL. A confusion on a headline: slide 3's "answered from memory", plus "Vedic" and
  "24 degrees apart as the cause". The caption's "hard topics with care" was vague.
- (d) PASS: "a person talking to me". Slide 3 "drifts a little toward a lecture".

## Fix list (ranked)
1. **Cover.** "isn't allowed to guess" leaves the object unclear. Say what the AI can't do in
   everyday words ("can't make things up"), and say it is free. Name the app in the body.
2. **Slide 3.** Cut the zodiac lesson (24 degrees, Western against Vedic counting). It reads
   as a lecture, and A cannot use it. Give the plain cause, which is in the article: chatbots
   don't calculate; they repeat what they learned from web pages that mix up Western and Indian
   astrology.
3. **Slide 2.** Say why two different Saturn signs matter: "that changes the whole reading".
4. **Slide 4.** "a normal program" works better as "my app calculates it, like a calculator".
5. **Slide 5.** "which parts of your chart it used" becomes "the planets it used".
6. **Slide 6.** Tell A how to get the app ("Try Panditji free"). Drop the "Tangent Labs" label.
   Keep one action: "link in bio".
7. **Caption.** Replace "hard topics with care" with the concrete examples from the article
   (predicting death, stock tips).
