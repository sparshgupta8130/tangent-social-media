# Message Brief: Men's Day x GTA 6 posters (IG carousel, 1:1)

**One-line message:** Men's Day and GTA 6 landed on the same day, so here's a funny, kind poster for each side of it.

**Primary audience:** A, especially Indian men aged about 22-40 who grew up on GTA, and the people who send things to them.
**Why they would care, in their words:** "Bro, slide 4 is literally us in the cyber café. Sending to the group."

**Format:** IG carousel, 1:1, 5 posters plus a cover and an end card.
- **Template / arc:** a numbered list, ordered from a laugh, to nostalgia, to an action.

**Voice and tone:** Sparsh, to his friends.
- **Tone:** person-witty, warm close. Logged for EXP-TONE.

**Look:** illustrated poster art in the style of a classic open-world crime game, set in everyday India.
- Bold outlines, cel shading, saturated sunset palette.
- Sparsh's call, 2026-11-19: the style is fine, as with the Ghibli trend.
- **Hard limits** (CLAUDE.md #1):
  - no Rockstar logo, "VI" lettering, signature font or characters;
  - no copied official art and no exact game screens;
  - no weapons or violence;
  - "GTA 6" and "Vice City" appear only as plain-text references.

## Facts used
- LEAVEMEALONE is a real GTA Vice City PC cheat that clears the wanted level. Sources: PC Gamer
  (pcgamer.com/gta-vice-city-cheats-pc) and GamesRadar (gamesradar.com/gta-vice-city-cheats),
  checked 2026-11-19 [MED-HIGH].
- Vice City released on PS2 in Oct 2002 and on PC in May 2003 (Giant Bomb release list,
  checked 2026-11-19). No year is printed on the slide, so "at 12" holds across ages.
- GTA 6 release date: 19 Nov 2026 (GamesRadar, TechRadar, PCGamesN, checked 2026-11-18).
- In GTA a dead player character respawns. That is game mechanics, said loosely as "a second life".

## Jargon and the plain replacement
- "respawn" → "you get a second life".
- "wanted level" → not needed on the slide. The joke works without it, and the caption explains.
