# Image prompts: Men's Day x GTA 6 posters (Nano Banana Pro, 1:1)

Generate at 1:1 (2K). Every image uses BASE plus its subject line. Headlines are added later in
code, so keep the **top ~30% of each frame as simple sky or plain background**.

**BASE:** Illustrated poster artwork in the style of classic open-world crime-game cover and
loading-screen art: bold clean ink outlines, cel-shaded comic realism, saturated sunset palette
(orange, magenta, teal), dramatic but friendly poses, painted gradient sky. Set in present-day
urban India with realistic Indian people, clothes and streets. Square composition, the top 30%
is open sky or plain background. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, logos, game logos,
signage, watermarks or UI. No weapons, no guns, no violence, no police chases, no real or famous
people, no characters from any existing game.
