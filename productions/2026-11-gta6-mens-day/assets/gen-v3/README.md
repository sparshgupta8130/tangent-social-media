# Generated images v3 ("GTA key-art"): Men's Day x GTA 6 posters (provenance record)

- **Model:** `gemini-3-pro-image` (Nano Banana Pro), called through the Gemini API `generateContent` with `responseModalities: [IMAGE]` and `imageConfig: {aspectRatio: "1:1", imageSize: "2K"}`. Output is 2048×2048 px.
- **Date:** 2026-11-18. Generated by Claude (Showrunner session) in autonomous mode for this one task. Target look: `../../STYLE-REFERENCE.md`. Spec: `../../image-prompts.md` (BASE v3 + subject lines). Slide copy: `../../carousel.md`.
- **Files:** `img-0N-cK.jpg` holds every candidate as JPEG q88. `pick/img-0N.png` is the chosen image per slide at full 2048 px, losslessly wrapping the API's output. `contact-sheet.jpg` shows the 7 labelled picks.
- **Attempts:** 36 images over 4 rounds. Images 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6 took 2 rounds (6 each), image 7 took 3 rounds (9), and image 5 took 4 rounds (12). Round 1 used the spec prompts unchanged on all 7.
- **Review:** I checked every candidate myself, first as triptychs and then frontrunners at 1024 px. I rejected any with text, letters, numbers, logos, brand names, signage or UI; bandanas or masks; borders; a top ~30% that is not sky or plain background (left ~45% for image 6); cartoon or caricature proportions, hatching or flat facets; weapons or police; resemblance to a famous person or game character; bad faces or hands; or a non-Indian setting.
- **Disclosure:** every image is fully AI-generated. No photo or third-party image was used as input. No GTA logo, font or character was requested or appears.

## What round 1 taught (applies to all)
- **Text leaks were the main failure.** These candidates had text: 2-c2 ("MARINE DRIVE", "MUMBAI" neon), 3-c2 ("CLINIC"), 4-c2 and 4-c3 (an Apple logo on the laptop; "BROADSHEET" on the newspaper), 5-c1 and 5-c2 (neon lettering; "PREPAID GAMES" posters), and 6-c2 ("METEOR" on the fuel tank).
- **BASE's "characters large in frame" drew people into image 7.** All of round 1 put couples on the seafront.
- **Round-1 rendering was flatter and more webcomic than the brand refs.** Round 2 on adds the **REALISM** line below to every image's prompt, so all 7 picks share it.

