- **Config:** `responseModalities: [IMAGE]`, `imageConfig: {aspectRatio: "1:1", imageSize: "2K"}`. Output is 2048×2048 px, returned by the API as JPEG.
- **Date:** 2026-11-18. Generated by Claude (Showrunner session) in autonomous mode for this one task. The spec is `../../image-prompts.md`, and the slide copy is `../../carousel.md`.
- **Files:** `img-0N-cK.jpg` holds every candidate, re-encoded as JPEG q88. `pick/img-0N.png` is the chosen image per slide at full 2048 px resolution: PNG, as asked, losslessly wrapping the API's JPEG. `contact-sheet.jpg` shows all 7 picks, labelled.
- **Review:** I looked at every candidate myself, as triptychs and as full-res crops of the lower 75% of each frontrunner. I rejected any with text, letters, numbers, logos, badges, signage or UI; weapons; resemblance to a real person or to existing game art and characters; bad hands or faces; a non-Indian setting; a drawn border or separate sky panel; or a top ~30% (left 45% on image 6) that was not open sky or plain background. Up to 4 extra rounds were allowed. The most any image used was 3 rounds (9 images).
- **Style:** every pick is a cel-shaded, ink-outlined poster illustration in the shared orange, magenta and teal sunset palette. They read as one set on the contact sheet. No image copies a real game's logo, font, characters or official art; the 'crime-game loading-screen' cue survives only as a general poster style. No weapons or violence.
- **Disclosure:** every image here is fully AI-generated. No photograph or third-party image was used as input.
