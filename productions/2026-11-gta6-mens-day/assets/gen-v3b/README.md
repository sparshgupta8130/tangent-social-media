## Picks
### img-02 → `c11`
- **Why it won:** a lean, realistic face with a defined jaw, cheekbones, stubble, a natural nose and a closed-lip smug half-smile with eyes closed. He has the banian, blue-grey checked pyjama shorts, red plastic chair, controller, steaming chai on a stool, black water tank, clothesline and the Marine Drive curve behind. The neon is plain light strips on a balcony. I checked the hands at full size and both are correct on the controller. I found no text, signs, logos or border at full size. Open sky reaches down to about 25% (his head top is at ~25%).
- **Weak spots:** (1) The palette is more violet and less peach-orange than old c4. It still matches images 1 and 6. (2) He is a bit smaller and leaner in frame than the old pick, so he reads less "dad bod" and more "fit guy". (3) Outlines are still visible: it is semi-realistic key art, not painterly realism.

### img-05 → `c12`
- **Why it won:** the boy (~12, green polo, beige CRT PC in a cramped café, with another customer behind him) has realistic child proportions and normal-sized eyes. The man (mid-30s, white shirt, lanyard with a blank card, sleeping baby on his shoulder, controller) has a realistic adult face. They share the side-parted black hair, thick straight brows and the mole on the left cheekbone, so they read as the same person. The open sunset sky with palms fills the top ~33% above both halves. I found no text: the café wall posters are abstract colour blobs only.
- **Weak spots:** (1) No hand supports the baby, since both of his hands are on the controller and the baby is perched on his shoulder. It reads fine at phone size but is physically unlikely. (2) The man has a second small mole near the beard. (3) The sky is still split by the panel divider, as in v3. (4) The boy's face is somewhat more youthful-stylised than the man's, though it is no longer the animated-film look. (5) There is a tiny dark speck (a bird) in the right half's sky.
