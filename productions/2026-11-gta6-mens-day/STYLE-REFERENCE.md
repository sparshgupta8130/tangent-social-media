# Style reference: the "GTA 6 key-art" look brands used (analysis, 2026-11-19)

Source: 6 Instagram screenshots Sparsh shared:
- exchange4media's roundup of brand GTA-6 posts: Burger King, Dunkin', KFC, Rapido, Axe;
- Haldiram's "Grand Theft Namkeen".

The screenshots are not stored in the repo: they are other brands' ads, logos and the GTA logo.
This file only describes the look.

## What the look is
| Element | What they do | v1 (ours) | S2 test (ours) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rendering | Semi-realistic digital painting: realistic adult anatomy and proportions, faces read as real people | comic, caricature-leaning | flat, geometric, stiff faces |
| Line | Clean, smooth, confident outlines of medium weight. No sketch marks or hatching | scratchy hatching, wobbly ink | crisp but too sparse |
| Shading | Cel shading in 2-3 crisp tones, plus soft airbrushed gradients on skin and cloth; glossy highlights | hatched | flat faceted planes (Rapido is the one ref that uses this) |
| Light | Strong warm sunset rim light on the figures' edges; neon or sunset reflections on wet ground | soft | flat |
| Palette | Violet → magenta → pink → peach sunset gradient sky with soft streaky clouds; purple shadows; teal accents | orange-teal | orange-magenta-teal |
| Setting | Palm silhouettes, a parking lot or seafront, storefronts, cars, wet reflective asphalt | dense Indian street | simple |
| Composition | Game-cover key art: 1-2 figures large in frame, walking toward camera or posing, eye-level or slightly low angle, cool confident expressions | figures smaller, busier scenes | ok |
| Type | GTA's own logo and font, with yellow-and-white outlined captions (Haldiram's) | ours: Anton | ours: Anton |

## What we copy and what we don't (CLAUDE.md #1)
- **Copy:** the rendering, line, shading, light, palette, setting mood and key-art composition.
  Style is not ownable, and that matches Sparsh's call.
- **Don't copy:**
  - the GTA logo, the "VI" lettering or GTA's signature font (Haldiram's used both; that's
    their legal team's call);
  - the bandana-masked couple. It is a direct lift of the two GTA VI lead characters, and it also
    reads as criminals;
  - any brand logos.
- **Type:** keep our own bold condensed type (Anton, white with a dark outline and a yellow
  accent). It sits in the same visual family without being their font.
- **Indian translation:** a coastal Indian city at sunset (a Marine Drive-style seafront with
  palms) gives the "Vice City" mood with our own people and places.
