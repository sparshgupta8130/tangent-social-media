How it works08 / 10

The AI isn’t allowed to guess.

1

Your phone does the maths.

It works out your chart from real astronomy. Planet positions are checked against NASA’s JPL and the Swiss Ephemeris; Vedic timings against Drik Panchang.

2

The AI only explains it.

The guide is an AI. It puts the computed numbers into words, and it can be wrong. For reflection and tradition, not advice.

3

Your chart stays on your phone.

Birth details, chats and notes live on your device. No ads, nothing for sale.
