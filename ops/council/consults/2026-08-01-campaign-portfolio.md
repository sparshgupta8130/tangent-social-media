## The question (verbatim)

> Sparsh is planning the first 6-week campaign (launch: tangentideas.ai IG + YouTube,
> ~zero followers). His immediate passion project: a web-series of AI-generated episodes on
> Param Vir Chakra recipients (India's highest wartime gallantry award) — story-driven,
> produced by his own AI pipeline. The photo-editing/agentic-craft lane (BET-1/2/3)
> launches in parallel.
>
> 1. **Brand architecture:** should the PVC series live ON tangentideas.ai (+ the YouTube
>    channel), or on its OWN handle/channel from day one? Weigh: the account premise is "AI
>    agents doing creative work" (PVC fits as 'watch AI create', subject-audience differs
>    sharply); the audience-mismatch death case (knowledge/virality/failure-modes.md,
>    handlytech); double-jeopardy/mental-availability logic for a ZERO-follower account
>    (does splitting surfaces before any audience exists violate growth economics?); the
>    masterbrand question (does "Tangent" span both, e.g., "Tangent Stories"?).
> 2. **Portfolio balance at launch:** what mix of craft-lane vs PVC-lane content in weeks
>    1-6 builds ONE coherent mental availability rather than two half-brands? Give a ratio
>    recommendation with reasoning.
> 3. **Distinctive-asset build:** which 2-3 assets (from distinctive-assets.md register)
>    should weeks 1-6 deliberately hammer so they start compounding from post 1, and what's
>    the per-post usage rule?
> 4. **One-line-description test:** write the target one-liner a stranger should say after
>    seeing 5 posts of the mixed slate — if you can't write one that covers both lanes,
>    that IS your architecture answer; say so.

## Position

**The architecture principle that answers all four questions: split where the premise breaks, not where the subject changes.** "Watch AI create, with taste" [REPO: strategy/STRATEGY.md §1] covers *making* the PVC series; it does not cover *watching* the PVC series. Everything premise-side stays on Tangent's surfaces; the story product gets its own home.

1. **Brand architecture:** PVC episodes launch on their OWN YouTube channel from day one, named for the series itself — not on tangentideas.ai's YT, and not as a "Tangent Stories" sub-brand. The making-of/pipeline content (C8's "how the AI built this") lives on tangentideas.ai + the main YT, where it is squarely on-premise. No new IG handle for the series yet — one new zero-follower surface, not two. "Made with Tangent's pipeline" is a credit line in descriptions/end-cards, not a channel identity; the masterbrand option stays open until either surface has fame worth transferring.

2. **Portfolio balance (weeks 1–6, tangentideas.ai):** minimum 4:1 craft-lane to PVC-bridge per week — at 5 slots: 4 craft + at most 1 pipeline-framed making-of reel; week 1 is 100% craft to set the grid's first impression. PVC-bridge posts slot as WORKHORSE and never displace the ≤2/week HERO ration, because BET-1/2/3's validation gates all need craft-lane volume inside this window. Episodes themselves cost the Studio no cadence ([SPARSH] masters) and run on the series channel at Sparsh's pipeline pace.

3. **Distinctive assets to hammer:** asset 6 (sliders-moving-by-themselves), asset 4 (the critic-as-character), asset 2 ("Machine craft, human taste," verbatim). Per-post rule: every craft demo reel carries all three in fixed form; every PVC-bridge post carries asset 2 verbatim + the wordmark, asset 6 only as genuine pipeline capture, and the critic only where a real verdict exists — never staged. Assets 1 and 3 (name/wordmark, gallery-white + Fraunces) are always-on frame hygiene, outside the hammer budget. No new recurring device joins the register in weeks 1–6; PVC-episode devices (title cards, narrator) belong to the series channel, not Tangent's register.

4. **One-line-description test:** with episodes on tangentideas.ai the test FAILS — the best honest line ("an account where AI edits photos and makes war-hero episodes") is an enumeration of two premises and drops the load-bearing craft/taste core. With the recommended slate (craft + pipeline-framed making-of) it PASSES: "the engineer teaching an AI taste — real photos, real craft — and now the AI is making a film." The test result IS the architecture answer, per the doctrine's own escape clause.

## Evidence

### Q1 — architecture: why the episodes get their own channel

- **The premise boundary is already drawn in the repo.** tangentideas.ai is "broader than
  photo editing by design, but never off the 'watch AI create, with taste' premise"
  [REPO: strategy/STRATEGY.md §1 property map]. C8 itself frames making-of/BTS content as
  what "bridges the PVC lane to tangentideas.ai's 'watch AI create' premise"
  [REPO: strategy/CANDIDATES.md C8] — i.e., the repo's own design says the *bridge* is the
  making-of, not the episodes. An episode's viewer is there for Major Somnath Sharma's
  story, not to watch an agent work; that viewer sits on none of the account's mapped
  category entry points [REPO: knowledge/marketing/positioning-doctrine.md, CEP map].
- **The audience-mismatch death case is account-level and self-reinforcing.** handlytech:
  989K followers, 1.0% median-views/follower — worst of 22 ranked accounts — because early
  off-topic hits kept re-priming visitor expectations against the account's real content
  [REPO: knowledge/virality/failure-modes.md §3; knowledge/marketing/growth-playbook.md,
  follower-quality doctrine]. The mechanism is viewer-expectation re-priming, which means
  the damage persists after the mixed posting stops. A zero-follower account is maximally
  exposed: its first weeks of followers and algorithm priming ARE the account's audience
  definition. Reversibility is asymmetric — merging a separate channel back later is cheap;
  un-training a mismatched follower base is the thing handlytech never managed
  ([EXTRAPOLATION] from the documented mechanism, flagged as inference, not a measured
  claim).
- **BET-5's own kill trigger already names this failure.** "Support reels dragging account
  efficiency toward the <5% warning band… → pause and re-architect (separate channel vs.
  tangentideas.ai is the standing brand-architecture question)" [REPO: strategy/BETS.md
  BET-5 kill trigger]. Starting merged means the separate channel is the *remedy after*
  paying the mismatch cost; starting separate skips the cost the trigger exists to catch.
- **The double-jeopardy objection cuts the other way here.** Double jeopardy says a small
  brand grows by penetration — reaching new light viewers — and Tangent at 0 followers
  must spend effort on reach [REPO: knowledge/marketing/brand-science.md §3]. But the
  standard argument against splitting surfaces (you dilute an audience and a cross-promo
  asset) assumes there is an audience to dilute; at zero followers on every surface,
  cross-promo leverage is ~nil, so the real split cost is production capacity and
  asset-rep concentration — and the episodes consume neither, because they are Sparsh's
  external pipeline entering as `[SPARSH]` masters [REPO: strategy/BETS.md BET-5 notes].
  Meanwhile hosting off-premise episodes on the main account does not add penetration *for
  the account's category* — a viewer recruited by a war-story episode is not mentally
  available for "how do I edit this photo" moments [REPO: brand-science.md §1: growth =
  being remembered at more *category* entry situations, not accumulating any viewers at
  all].
- **Depth-over-breadth doctrine, honestly weighed against the position.** The
  growth-playbook's platform-portfolio rule is to resist new surfaces before existing ones
  function [REPO: knowledge/marketing/growth-playbook.md, platform-portfolio risk]. I am
  recommending a new surface anyway, and the tension is real. The resolution: that
  doctrine protects the *reach job* of existing surfaces; putting the episodes on the main
  YT does not deepen that surface's job (native re-cuts + finished-art spectacle of the
  craft lane [REPO: STRATEGY.md §1]) — it gives the surface a second, unrelated job, which
  is the thing the four-surfaces-one-job-each rule exists to prevent
  [REPO: positioning-doctrine.md, brand architecture doctrine].
- **The masterbrand question ("Tangent Stories") — both schools point the same way, which
  is rare.** The Sharp/Romaniuk side: an asset counts only at high fame × high uniqueness,
  and "a new device competes for the same fame-building budget as the existing 7" — every
  Tangent asset is UNBUILT today, so there is no fame to transfer, only a budget to split
  [REPO: knowledge/marketing/distinctive-assets.md, header + checklist rule 2; CANON:
  Romaniuk, *Building Distinctive Brand Assets*, 2018]. The positioning side: a brand
  survives by owning ONE distinct position in the mind [CANON: Ries & Trout, *Positioning:
  The Battle for Your Mind*, 1981, via REPO: brand-science.md §2] — stretching one
  zero-fame name across "gallery-grade craft" and "war-hero drama" builds neither
  position. These two schools disagree on most things [REPO: brand-science.md §2,
  [CONTESTED]]; their convergence here is the strongest signal in this memo. The doctrine
  also flatly bars a third variant name on our own surfaces ("never a third variant name
  for YouTube or email" [REPO: positioning-doctrine.md, naming rule]) — "Tangent Stories"
  as a channel identity would need an explicit doctrine amendment, which only Sparsh +
  Showrunner can make, at a weekly review [REPO: strategy/STRATEGY.md preamble;
  `ops/ROLES.md` decision right (f) as cited there].
- **No new IG handle for the series.** BET-5 is YT-long-form-primary by design (story/
  spectacle → long-form: Law 10's genre-lane rule [REPO: knowledge/virality/laws.md Law
  10; strategy/BETS.md BET-5]). Opening a second zero-follower IG account adds a fourth
  cold surface with no evidence base; revisit when the channel has retention data.
- **UNKNOWN — research gap:** no corpus case or whitelisted literature in the repo covers
  a solo creator launching two zero-follower channels simultaneously (split-attention
  cost on early medians). Logged for `/weekly-review`.
- **UNKNOWN — research gap:** whether YouTube's channel-level recommendation treats
  mixed-genre uploads adversely — no T1/T2 source in the repo; handlytech is an IG
  viewer-expectation case, not YT recommender mechanics. This memo's Q1 position
  deliberately rests on the expectation-priming mechanism and premise doctrine, not on any
  algorithm claim.

### Q2 — portfolio balance: why 4:1, and why week 1 is craft-pure

- **The craft gates need the slots.** BET-3's gate needs ≥8 HERO demo reels; BET-1's gate
  reads at post 20 in the lane; BET-2's funnel gate reads across the first 5 funnel posts
  [REPO: strategy/BETS.md BET-1/2/3]. At the launch-surge ceiling of 2 HERO/week
  [REPO: strategy/STRATEGY.md §7; knowledge/brand/content-architecture.md, tier table],
  the 6-week window is approximately what those gates require with NO slot leakage. Every
  main-account slot given to PVC beyond the bridge ration delays the date any craft bet
  becomes readable.
- **One mental availability = one premise answered repeatedly.** Mental availability grows
  by being remembered at more category entry situations [REPO: brand-science.md §1], and
  the account's CEP map is entirely craft-lane situations ("how do I edit this photo,"
  "AI did something insane," "what's Claude actually capable of")
  [REPO: positioning-doctrine.md, CEP map]. Operational rule carried into the ratio: a
  PVC-bridge brief must name which mapped entry point it captures, like any other brief
  [REPO: positioning-doctrine.md — "a brief that can't name which entry-point situation
  it's capturing has already failed selector criterion 1"]. Pipeline-framed making-of
  answers "AI did something insane" / "what's Claude actually capable of"; a story-first
  trailer answers no mapped entry point → it belongs on the series channel, and the ratio
  stays honest because the rule polices itself per-brief.
- **Why not 50/50 or 2:1:** consistency compounds via mere exposure — repeated exposure to
  the same format/motif increases liking pre-consciously [REPO: brand-science.md §6;
  CANON: Zajonc 1968, via that section]. Two half-premises in equal rotation reset each
  other's compounding. The Binet & Field 60/40 finding points the same direction
  (most output builds one brand; a minority does a different short-term job) but it is
  budget research on established brands — [EXTRAPOLATION], directional only, per the
  file's own flag [REPO: brand-science.md §6]. The specific 4:1 number is my judgment
  inside the cited cadence bands (3–5 reels/week, floor 2 [REPO: STRATEGY.md §7]), stated
  as a starting allocation for `/weekly-review` to tune against medians — not a law.
- **Week 1 craft-pure, first bridge post in week 2 at the earliest:** bio-line and grid
  first impression are what move profile-visit→follow conversion [REPO:
  knowledge/marketing/growth-playbook.md, funnel table], and the launch guidance already
  in the backlog says front-load the lane's quality before going live ("~3 HERO reels +
  the series' first two weeks ready BEFORE" launch) [REPO: strategy/CANDIDATES.md,
  sequencing notes]. The first ~9-tile grid should read as one premise to a cold profile
  visitor.
- **Tier discipline for the bridge posts:** WORKHORSE, inside an owned skeleton (making-of
  fits format (a)'s demo grammar or (b)'s numbered wrapper), never the HERO ration —
  "filler never replaces hero quality" is constitutional and the HERO budget is the
  BET-3 experiment [REPO: content-architecture.md, tier table + format→tier mapping;
  CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 8].
- **Episodes cost the ratio nothing:** they enter as `[SPARSH]` masters on the separate
  channel [REPO: strategy/BETS.md BET-5 notes], so the 4:1 governs only tangentideas.ai +
  main-YT re-cuts. Every episode-linked item still takes its own mandatory `/consult`
  before scripting (accuracy/likeness/respect — [REPO: strategy/BETS.md BET-5 standing
  conditions; strategy/CANDIDATES.md C7]); this memo does not pre-clear any episode.

### Q3 — distinctive assets: why 6, 4, and 2

- **Asset 6 (sliders-moving-by-themselves):** "Tangent's most defensibly unique asset —
  nobody in the corpus has this pipeline… uniqueness starts near-ceiling"
  [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, asset 6]. It is also the differentiation claim we can
  film rather than assert — the exact case the positioning school's argument was built
  for [REPO: brand-science.md §2, Council synthesis]. Highest-uniqueness asset gets the
  most reps while fame is the only variable to move.
- **Asset 4 (critic-as-character):** "the highest-leverage unbuilt asset" — serves
  disclosure doctrine, the confidence-gap persona, and fame simultaneously; "likely
  fastest asset to reach high fame+uniqueness" [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, asset 4].
  Recurring named characters also compound parasocial trust + mere exposure
  [REPO: knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md, naming conventions].
- **Asset 2 (the line, verbatim):** a verbal asset compounds only unparaphrased — "reuse
  the exact phrase, never a paraphrase" [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, asset 2], and
  paraphrase is an automatic G3 checklist FAIL [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, checklist
  rule 4]. It is also the only one of the three that travels effortlessly into PVC-bridge
  posts, making it the connective tissue of the mixed slate.
- **Per-post usage rules (the operational ask):**
  - Craft demo reel (format a): asset 6 as genuine screen capture (never mocked/
    speed-ramped to read as staged [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, asset 6 protect]) +
    asset 4 at its fixed label/typography/beat position [REPO: tangent-voice.md — a
    recurring device is "reused identically across posts once adopted"] + asset 2
    verbatim in the caption.
  - Series/workflow post (formats b/c): asset 4 only where a real G1/G2 rejection exists
    — a staged verdict is a disclosure violation, not just brand dilution
    [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, asset 4 protect] — plus asset 2 verbatim in caption.
  - PVC-bridge making-of: asset 2 verbatim + wordmark treatment; asset 6 only as genuine
    capture of the actual episode pipeline; no critic unless the pipeline's gate verdict
    is real.
  - Assets 1 (name, never abbreviated) and 3 (gallery-white + Fraunces, never-use list)
    are always-on frame hygiene on every post [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, assets 1, 3;
    tangent-voice.md] — they are the frame, not the hammer, so they do not consume the
    2–3-asset focus budget.
  - No new recurring device enters the register in weeks 1–6: "a new device competes for
    the same fame-building budget as the existing 7" [REPO: distinctive-assets.md,
    checklist rule 2]. PVC-episode devices (title card, narrator voice, episode
    numbering) are the series channel's own future register, kept deliberately separate.
  - Register-status note per Contract (d): any status change (UNBUILT → EMERGING) lands
    only via `/weekly-review`, never mid-campaign [REPO: distinctive-assets.md, status
    key].

### Q4 — the one-liner, attempted honestly

- **The test:** after 5 posts, does a stranger land on "the engineer teaching an AI taste
  — real photos, real craft"? Pass/fail, not a spectrum; dropping a load-bearing element
  is a FAIL [REPO: positioning-doctrine.md, one-line-description test].
- **Mixed slate WITH episodes on tangentideas.ai:** best honest attempt — "an account
  where an AI edits photos and also makes episodes about Indian war heroes." That is an
  enumeration of two premises, and it loses the craft/taste core entirely. I cannot write
  a single non-enumerated line that covers episodes-as-content; per the question's own
  framing, that IS the architecture answer, and it agrees with Q1.
- **Recommended slate (craft + pipeline-framed making-of):** "the engineer teaching an AI
  taste — real photos, real craft — and now the AI is making a film." The canonical core
  survives intact; the series appears as the pipeline's next season, which is exactly the
  multi-year arc STRATEGY already claims ("photographs now; video, music, apps as later
  seasons" [REPO: strategy/STRATEGY.md §2]). The PVC series, framed as *what the pipeline
  is making*, is the first "later season" arriving early — on-premise, provided the
  audience only ever meets it on Tangent surfaces through the pipeline frame.

## What would change my mind

- **Q1 (the split):** `[OWN-DATA]` from the first 3 pipeline-framed bridge posts on
  tangentideas.ai showing median ≥ account median efficiency AND clean sentiment
  classification (BET-5's own draft gate numbers [REPO: strategy/BETS.md BET-5]) — real
  overlap between the craft audience and the PVC-story audience beyond what the mismatch
  model predicts — would justify folding the series' promotion (not the episodes) much
  deeper into the main account. Conversely, if Sparsh's ops capacity cannot hold the
  series channel above a dead-channel state (episodes stalling for months), a dormant
  branded surface is worse proof than none — I would then fold episodes into the main YT
  as a clearly-labeled playlist and accept the mixed-channel cost knowingly.
- **Q2 (the ratio):** two consecutive `/weekly-review`s showing PVC-bridge posts at or
  above craft-lane medians with account efficiency holding ≥10%
  [REPO: strategy/STRATEGY.md §6 band] → loosen toward 3:1. Bridge posts tripping the
  efficiency slide or the off-pillar-hits warning of the handlytech triad
  [REPO: knowledge/virality/failure-modes.md §3] → tighten to 0 and revisit architecture.
- **Q3 (the asset set):** comment/DM data showing an unregistered device earning
  unprompted recognition faster than assets 4/6 (fame evidence per the register's own
  measures [REPO: distinctive-assets.md]) → promote it into the hammer set at weekly
  review, dropping one, never expanding to four.
- **Q4:** if Sparsh redefines the account premise itself (a STRATEGY §2 change, his +
  Showrunner's joint right at weekly review), the test target changes and this memo's
  architecture logic must be re-run against the new line — the test, not my answer to it,
  is the invariant.
