## 1. Cadence decision + capacity math

### The decision

**W1 dark (bank), W2–W4 time-boxed launch surge at 5 posts/week, W5–W6 steady state at 3–4/week.**
Go-live: **Mon 2026-08-10.**

- **W1 (Aug 3–9) publishes nothing.** CANDIDATES' sequencing note is explicit: ~3 HERO reels + the series' first two weeks banked BEFORE going live — "the series is the net under the heroes" (CANDIDATES, mined from 04 §4). Launching Aug 3 with an empty bank violates that and burns the launch surge on unbanked quality. Going live Aug 10 also puts Independence Day (Sat Aug 15) inside launch week — the surge's elevated cadence absorbs the conditional PVC slot without displacing anything.
- **W2–W4 (Aug 10–30) is a launch surge, but NOT the full band.** STRATEGY §7 permits "up to daily series episodes + 2 HERO/week." I am deliberately picking **5/week, not 9/week** — capacity math below. The surge is 21 days, inside the ≤30-day / once-per-quarter box, with the §7 health guard live: any G2 FIX backlog or a BLOCKED-ON-SPARSH item older than 3 days auto-drops the week to steady state. Cadence bends before craft (CLAUDE.md non-negotiable 8).
- **W5–W6 revert to steady state (3–4 reels/week)** — the honest post-surge floor this capacity can hold indefinitely, comfortably above STRATEGY §7's floor of 2/week.

### Why the full surge is rejected (the math)

Sparsh's budget: **~300 min/week of weekday attention** (5 × ~60 min) + a **4–8h weekend block that is spoken for by the Param Vir Chakra pipeline build** (C7 is his external production lane). Planning assumption: **weekends contribute ≤0 min to the social team** except a one-time W1 raw-supply session; if PVC finishes early one weekend, that's a windfall, never a plan (Law 11 discipline applied to his time, not just views).

Per-item Sparsh cost (checkpoints only — the agent team scripts, produces, cuts, and runs G1/G2/G3; his hour is approvals, raw supply, and SHIP):

| HERO reel (C1) | 5 | 10 | 15 | 5 | **~40 min** |
| Series episode (C2) | 2 (batched weekly) | — | 8 | 5 | **~15 min** |

**Full-band surge costed honestly:** 7 series eps (105) + 2 HERO (80) + 1 funnel (25) + rituals (35) + raws (20) + re-cut checks (~25) ≈ **290/300 min — 97% utilization, zero slack.** One FIX round (+15–30 min, and G1/G2 allow up to two) or one PVC-heavy week breaks it, and the failure mode is exactly the one STRATEGY §7 names: quality collapse showing up in the medians within weeks. A 97%-loaded launch is a plan to miss.

**Chosen surge (5/week) costed:** 3 series eps (45) + 1–2 HERO (40–80) + 1 funnel or conditional slot (25–75) + rituals (35) + raws (20) + re-cut checks (~20) ≈ **185–250 min — 62–83% utilization**, leaving real slack for fix rounds, a news window (C4), and the PVC conditional. This is also consistent with the honest ops model: solo + agents ≈ Riley's 4–5 crafted posts/week [CORPUS: synthesis/02 §7, cited in STRATEGY §7].
