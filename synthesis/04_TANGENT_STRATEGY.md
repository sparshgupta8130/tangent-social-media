# Tangent — Channel & Page Launch Strategy

Built ONLY on Gate-C-cleared findings (01-03) + demand evidence (research/04). Existing assets:
tangent.gallery (12-plate exhibit, classy/minimal brand), instagram_logo.png, profile images,
Post_Videos/, the fine-art-photo-editing skill (5 rounds of critic-hardened craft), the working
Claude→Lightroom agentic pipeline.

## 1. Positioning

**"Watch AI learn taste."** An engineer built an AI art gallery end-to-end — his own photographs,
edited to gallery grade by Claude driving Lightroom, with a brutal critic loop — and shows every
step. One sentence bio: *"I teach AI to edit like a master. My camera, my code, my taste."*

Why this wins per the evidence: (a) it uses demo-in-motion grammar — PROVEN for tool/app demos
(01 §1.5) but a stated BET for art content specifically: no one in the corpus has run this format
on art, and WS-3's demand evidence is proxy-based. The launch month is the validation experiment,
with explicit pivot gates in §5;
(b) it's verifiable proof, not assertion — the credential substitute (02 §6); (c) it sits on
WS-1+2+3 simultaneously; (d) it is the exact anti-"AI slop" position the backlash map demands
(03): disclosed, own-work, human-taste-gated. The unfair advantages nobody in the corpus can
copy: the pipeline exists and is filmable; the 12-plate exhibit is finished proof-of-work; the
whole gallery's creation is itself a content archive.

## 2. Content architecture (key vs filler — per 02 §5)

**HERO (reach engine, 2/week):** "Claude edits" agentic demo reels — screen-capture of sliders
moving, masks drawing themselves, the critic rejecting a draft, the final reveal. Template
(per 01 §1.1-1.5, Devin/Riley grammar): 0-1s = finished plate or mid-motion sliders (result
first); 3-8s = the ask ("turn this flat raw into a gallery plate"); then granular visible
state-changes every 5-10s with ONE honest imperfection beat (critic rejection = our native
"error-and-fix"); escalate to the reveal; close the loop with before/after; one absurd/delight
beat where natural (the critic's brutal one-liners are quotable). 25-40s. No preamble, ever.

**WORKHORSE A (cold-start series, daily for launch month):** "30 Days of Machine Craft" —
numbered series (Harper mechanics, 01 §1.7): each episode = one raw → one finished plate + ONE
teachable taste principle (the "why" in plain language, no jargon — WS-5 tone). Series =
follow-conversion wrapper that catches whatever hero reel hits.

**WORKHORSE B (Claude-for-creatives, 1/week):** exact workflows — "the prompt that grades color
like a colorist," Lightroom+Claude setups, preset-pack demos. Keyword-gated deliverable each time
(mechanics below). This is the WS-2 lane and the funnel engine.

**FILLER (cadence insurance, 1/week, priced as filler per 02 §5):** news-response within 0-4
days on Adobe/AI-imaging/Claude releases (01 §1.8) — our take demonstrated on our own raws, never
a talking-head recap. NO podcast-clip-type content (reliably sub-median corpus-wide).

**LONG-FORM FLAGSHIP (monthly, YouTube):** the WS-4 lane — "One plate, start to finish" (10-15
min: raw scout → agent build → critic war → print), and quarterly spectacle ("An AI curated its
own gallery show"). Art spectacle wants long-form (Curious Refuge lane-inversion, 01 §1.10).

Cadence, reconciled: LAUNCH MONTH (time-boxed 30 days) = 1 series episode/day + 2 HERO/week +
1 WORKHORSE-B/week. STEADY STATE = 4 reels/week (2 HERO, 1 WORKHORSE-B, 1 FILLER/news) + 1
long-form flagship/month — the honest solo+agents ops model (02 §7). Lane-inversion resolution
(01 §1.10): PROCESS demos are native short-form (the proven demo genre); FINISHED-ART spectacle
belongs to the long-form flagships — the two lanes split exactly along the corpus's genre rule
rather than fighting it. Plan on the MEDIAN; treat spikes as windfalls (01 §1.11).

## 3. Distribution & funnel mechanics

- **Platform split:** Instagram Reels = primary reach surface for the demo grammar (Devin's
  >1,000x IG-over-YT delta on identical content); YouTube = Shorts re-cut natively (not blind
  cross-posts — 01 §1.10) + the long-form flagships + the 35+ audience (52% of YT). TikTok:
  deferred (sebastien's 558K ghost-twin warning).
- **Keyword-DM funnel on every WORKHORSE B and eligible HERO post** (01 §1.6): "comment CRAFT and
  I'll DM you the exact prompt+preset." Deliverable must be real and instant (compliance line).
  Capture → email list → future: preset packs, the editing-skill course, plugin/SaaS if AutoEdit-
  style opportunity firms up, prints via tangent.gallery.
- **CTA grammar per platform:** keyword-DM on IG; "full build on the channel" on YT shorts →
  long-form (Herk's split, ig_nateherkai).
- **Sponsorships:** only inside our own template (Harper's ClaudePartner precedent vs her
  ServiceNow flop; Varun's 10-15x ad penalty — 01 §1.9). Never buy views (kallaway/handlytech
  lessons: bought reach poisons the audience signal and the trust ledger).

## 4. Launch sequence (first 90 days)

Week 1-2: publish 3 HERO reels + Day 1-14 of the series (front-load quality; the series is the
net under the heroes). Week 3-13: steady-state cadence; every Adobe/Anthropic release gets the
0-4-day news-response treatment; first monthly long-form at week 4. Growth accounting weekly
(pivot-on-signal, 02 §1): which pillar over/under-performs its median → double the winner,
never rebuild around an off-pillar spike (handlytech law). Collab lane: O'Reilly-style collabs
underperform (Harper data) — prefer cross-appearances in the Claude/creative-tools communities
(r/ClaudeAI, photography subreddits) where WS-2 demand already concentrates.

## 5. Metrics dashboard (medians, not tops — 01 §1.11, 02 §9)

Weekly: median reel views; median/follower efficiency (healthy: 10-15% while small; 20%+ excellent. The corpus's
small-account ceiling case — Devin's 156% — is a funded 3-person team mid-product-launch: an
existence proof for the lane, NOT a solo benchmark); like-rate band check (1-10% = healthy organic); comment:
like ratio on funnel posts (>0.5 = mechanic working; Devin/Herk benchmark); DM-delivery→email
conversion; follow conversion during series vs off-series. Flop autopsies use the views×LR
decomposition (01 Law 0.2) — hook failure vs craft failure get different fixes. Monthly: audience
-match audit (are new followers engaging with PILLAR content? — the handlytech early-warning).

**Launch-month validation gates (the demo-in-motion-for-art bet, per §1):** after ≥8 HERO posts,
if the HERO median underperforms the series-episode median, the bet is failing → shift HERO
effort into long-form spectacle (the corpus-proven art lane) and demote reels to before/after
teasers. If comment:like on funnel posts stays <0.2, the keyword mechanic isn't landing with an
art audience → test gating a different deliverable class (presets vs prompts vs process PDFs)
before abandoning it.

## 6. Risks & standing rules

1. **Backlash:** disclosure-first framing in bio and every video; own raws only; never IP
   mashups; "human taste is the gate" said explicitly and often. (03 backlash map.)
2. **Boost temptation:** never — three corpus case studies show it buys dead audiences and
   media-kit fiction.
3. **Off-brand virality:** if a non-art post spikes, enjoy it and return to pillar — do not
   rebuild (handlytech autopsy).
4. **Burnout/quality collapse:** filler tier exists so hero quality never gets sacrificed to
   cadence; staffed-cadence envy is banned (02 §7).
5. **Single-platform fragility:** the email list from the DM funnel is the only owned asset;
   grow it from day one.
6. **The AI-topic reach ceiling (Skill Leap law — 01 §1.2):** never lead with "AI" as the
   promise; lead with the universally-legible payoff (a breathtaking photograph, an impossible
   transformation) and let the AI be the twist. Our hooks sell the image, not the technology —
   pure-AI framing caps reach even when resonance is high, and Tangent is otherwise 100%
   AI-topic content.

## 7. What only Sparsh can ship (the moat, restated)

A real, working, filmable agentic art pipeline; a finished gallery as proof; engineering
credibility to teach the Claude lane; full ownership of every pixel shown; and the story arc —
"an engineer taught an AI taste" — which is a SERIES, not a gimmick, with years of episodes in
it (photos → video → music → apps, each a new season of the same story).
