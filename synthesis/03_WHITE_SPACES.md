# White Spaces — Where Demand Exceeds Supply

Method: corpus supply-side gaps (18 dossiers + 24-account harvest + discovery rosters) crossed
with independent demand-side evidence (research/04_whitespace_demographics.md — platform
demographics, search/survey trends, community growth; spot-verified). Ranked by
(demand strength × supply gap × fit to owner's provable capability ÷ backlash risk).

## Ranked white spaces

**WS-1. AI + Lightroom/Photoshop fine-art editing workflows.** Demand: tens of millions of
hashtagged posts, r/photography ~5.5M members, sustained search interest in AI photo editing
[MED-HIGH]. Supply: ZERO human creators above 200K IG in the subniche (discovery sweep found only
faceless gallery pages); Devin Huynh — the only adjacent Claude+Adobe creator — is at 85K with the
study's highest reach efficiency (156%), proving the demand reaches non-followers hard.
SURVIVORSHIP GUARD: Devin is the corpus's ONLY sub-300K account and is a funded 3-person team
riding a live product launch — treat his 156% as an existence proof of demand in this lane, not
as a solo-creator benchmark. The one
big "editing" adjacent account (elevenpercent) is video-editing, not photography. VERDICT: the
single clearest demand-supply gap in the study, and it is exactly the owner's proven capability.

**WS-2. Claude-specific creative workflows ("Claude for creatives").** Demand: r/ClaudeAI
250K→1M in 13 months (verified, accelerating +37%/90d); Claude Code awareness ~doubled YoY.
Supply: Claude content that exists is coding/automation (Saraev's "free Claude Code" outliers,
Riley's vibe-coding); NO dedicated creative-workflow supply found. Adjacent proof: Devin's
"comment Claude" top-5 cluster; Harper's #ClaudePartner posts sitting in her top-5 (sponsor-fit
precedent — Anthropic pays creator-educators). VERDICT: high-growth, near-empty lane adjacent to
WS-1; also the owner's literal daily toolchain.

**WS-3. Agentic creation as spectacle — "watch an AI agent edit like a master."** Demand proxy:
demo-in-motion is the proven grammar of the niche's best reach outliers (Riley 16x median;
Devin 156% efficiency), and "AI agent" is the cycle's hottest term. Supply: agent-demos exist ONLY
for apps/automation; nobody shows an agent doing ART with visible tool-driving (masks, curves,
sliders moving on their own, critic loops). The owner's pipeline — Claude driving Lightroom
through a browser with a critic gate — is, per this study, unique and directly filmable. VERDICT:
the differentiation play; merges WS-1's audience with WS-2's wave. Risk: "fully autonomous"
framing touches backlash — mitigate via the craft/curation framing (below).

**WS-4. Original-aesthetic AI art spectacle (long-form lane).** Curious Refuge proved AI-art
spectacle draws millions (3-4.4M) but their formula runs on borrowed nostalgia IP (legally hedged
"fan-made parody"), is dormant 2-3 years, and fatigues by iteration three. Nobody has replaced
IP-mashup curiosity with ORIGINAL-work spectacle (before/after transformations, impossible
edits, gallery-grade reveals). Genre rule from their data: this content wants LONG-FORM (their
median long-form beat their best Short). VERDICT: Tangent's flagship lane — owns all rights,
immune to the IP treadmill.

**WS-5. AI for the 35+ / AI-skeptic creative.** Demand: 35+ is 52% of YouTube's ad-reachable
audience (vs 35% of Instagram's) and the niche's youth-coded style ignores it; Pew: creative-AI
usage is gender-balanced (24%/23%) while sentiment is not (+16 men / −10 women) — a
confidence/tone gap, not interest gap. Supply: tiffintech is the only tonal outlier, and her
differentiation is business-mix, not content. VERDICT: not a separate content lane — a TONE AND
PLATFORM decision (YouTube long-form + calm, craft-first, jargon-free voice) layered on WS-1/4.

**WS-6. India / non-English (deferred).** Largest audience on both platforms, most AI-optimistic;
but 61% of Indian YT watch-time is regional-language, and the corpus's India winners (Sisinty,
Mayya, 100xEngineers) already own English-India AI-generalist. VERDICT: revisit after
establishment; a Hindi/regional fine-art-editing series would be genuinely unserved but is a
scaling move, not a launch move.

## The backlash map (positioning constraint on ALL of the above)

Every documented anti-AI-art flashpoint targeted UNDISCLOSED or FULLY-AUTONOMOUS generation:
Cara's 650K-in-a-week exodus (scraping/undisclosed), SDCC/FanX bans (generated art sold as art),
Meta's own July 2026 Muse Image retreat (user-photo consent — the sharpest flashpoint sits
exactly at photography×AI×Instagram). NONE targeted disclosed, human-curated, tool-assisted
craft. The owner's actual pipeline (own raws, multi-round critique, human taste as the gate,
full disclosure) is structurally on the safe side — but only if the positioning SAYS so, loudly
and first. "AI slop" is the enemy brand; "machine craft, human taste, my own photographs" is the
counter-position. Never hide the AI; never claim the AI is unsupervised when it isn't; never
touch other people's images or IP.
