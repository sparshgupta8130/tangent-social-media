## 1. Summary
- He was NOT a prince or heir of Tibet. Ardussi: a "scion of the rGya hierarchs of Ra-lung", already installed as Ralung hierarch; his rival Pagsam Wangpo (1593-1641) was a bastard son of the Phyongs-rgyas myriarch. [HISTORY]
- No uncle and no assassination attempt appears in any source read. The threat was the Tsang ruler's court, which demanded the sacred Kharsapani image. UNKNOWN beyond that.
- He left in 1616 (Ardussi), not in the 15th-16th century. Born 1594.
- The "dragon dream" is not found in any source read. Popular sources (tourism) say a dream/vision of a RAVEN flying south, read as a sign from the protector Mahakala (Mahakali in one source). [TRADITION], weakly sourced.
- "Druk" comes from the Drukpa school and the monastery Tsangpa Gyare named after thunder (traditional account); it predates 1616. The Druk Yul name for the state of Bhutan is, per one Bhutanese site, formal only c. 1900.

## Key quotes (Ardussi 2004, JBS 11 PDF, page numbers are journal pages)
- p.12-13: two rival candidates for rebirth of Padma-dkar-po: Zhabs-drung (1594-1651) and dPag-bsam dBang-po (1593-1641) "a bastard son of the powerful 'Phyongs-rgyas myriarch".
- p.13: "In 1616 he decided to take refuge with his patrons in what is now the state of Bhutan."
- p.14: "in 1618 Phun-tshogs rNam-rgyal launched an army into Bhutan. However, the Zhabs-drung won this battle."
- p.14: 1623 three-year retreat in a cave north of Thimphu; in "the eleventh month of the Wood-Ox year (1625/26)" he announced a new government; state founded 1625/26.
- p.18: "his death in 1651 was kept a secret for more than fifty years".
