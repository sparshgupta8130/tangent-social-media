# 02 Buddhas of the Three Times and the Bhutanese altar triad (DRAFT 1)

Status: DRAFT 1, 2026-11-11. Sources opened and read are listed with URLs. Anything not opened is marked UNKNOWN. Tags: [TRADITION] doctrinal/legendary, [HISTORY] documented.

## 1. Summary
1. "Buddhas of the three times" are three individual Buddhas of successive ages (past Dipamkara, present Shakyamuni, future Maitreya), not three "types". Shared across Tibetan Buddhism, not unique to Bhutan.
2. Posture and gesture: in the Gelug painting convention described by Himalayan Art Resources, Shakyamuni touches the earth; Dipamkara and Maitreya both show the teaching gesture. So "each has its own gesture" is only partly true: two of the three share one.
3. The altar triad a visitor most often meets in Bhutan is Shakyamuni flanked by Guru Rinpoche and Zhabdrung, a different triad from the three times.
4. Guru Rinpoche is himself called "Buddha of the three times" in a famous prayer, a likely source of confusion.
5. Buddha Dordenma (Thimphu) is Shakyamuni; height sources disagree (54 m vs 201 ft).
