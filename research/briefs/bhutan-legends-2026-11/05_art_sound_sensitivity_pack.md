## PART C. Rules, permissions, sensitivities

### C1. Regulation of depiction; applicability to a foreigner publishing an animation from abroad
All of the following are `[SEARCH-LEAD]` (search summaries; PDFs not opened because www.bicma.gov.bt was blocked):
- BICMA "Rules and Regulations on Content 2019", issued under Section 58 of the 2018 Act, reportedly says content must be measured against social, religious, political and cultural requirements, and must avoid content that incites religious, ethnic, regional or communal conflict or is offensive to public feeling. https://www.bicma.gov.bt/data/publications/rules-regulations-guidelines/Rules_and_Regulations_on_Content_2019.pdf
- National Film Review Board certification reportedly checks that films are not antithetical to Bhutan's social and cultural values (2023 and 2025 certification rules). https://www.bicma.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Rules-Governing-Examination-and-Certification-of-Films-2025.pdf
- Bhutan Filming Regulation 2025 reportedly took effect 19 March 2025, covers filming activity "in Bhutan", names BICMA as sole permit authority for foreign filmmakers, requires a local counterpart, and has a restricted-locations section (8.3). https://www.bicma.gov.bt/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Bhutan-Filming-Regulation-2025.pdf
- BICMA reportedly clarified that music videos and vlogging need no filming permit: https://thebhutanese.bt/?p=44126
- Precedent: film "Hema Hema" reportedly denied certification over use of Lha-tshog masks, deemed culturally inappropriate by BICMA after review by the Department of Culture: https://www.bbs.bt/news/?p=65932 and https://globalvoices.org/2017/01/19/bhutans-authorities-ban-film-for-misusing-religious-masks-on-screen/

Clearly stated (still to be verified by opening the PDFs): the filming regulation concerns filming in Bhutan; film certification concerns films in Bhutan's system.

UNKNOWN: whether any rule regulates depiction of religious figures, the national flag or emblem, the royal family or dzongs in media; whether any of these rules apply to a foreign creator publishing an animation on YouTube from abroad with no footage shot in Bhutan. No source found states either way. Recommend asking BICMA and the Department of Culture in writing.

### C5. Risk reduction (editorial recommendation, not sourced)
These follow from the brief's own suggestions and the unresolved C1 to C4 gaps, not from verified findings:
