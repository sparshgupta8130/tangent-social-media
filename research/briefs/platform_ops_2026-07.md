## 1. INSTAGRAM REELS SPECS (2026)

### Length
- Recording in-app camera: capped around 3 minutes. [MED] — [How Long Can Instagram Reels Be in 2026?](https://www.sellerpic.ai/blog/instagram-reel-size) (accessed 2026-07-31)
- Uploading a pre-recorded file: Instagram now accepts much longer uploads (reports of 15–20 min in extended rollouts), but **content that actually gets pushed to non-followers via Explore/Reels tab is still effectively capped at ~3 minutes** — most creators treat 3 min as the real ceiling. [MED] — [Instagram Reel Length 2026](https://flowshorts.app/blog/instagram-reel-length) (2026-07-31)
- History (for context): 15s (Aug 2020) → 30s (2021) → 60s (Jan 2022) → 90s (mid-2022, held through 2023) → 3 min (Jan 2025). [MED] — multiple consistent secondary sources (2026-07-31)
- **Practical recommendation**: target 7–15 seconds for hook-driven Shorts-style content, hard cap at 90s for algorithmic reach unless doing an explicit long-form-style Reel. [LOW/UNVERIFIED] — inferred from creator-economy blog consensus, not an official Meta figure.

### Aspect ratio & safe zones (1080×1920 canvas)
- Standard export: **1080 × 1920 px, 9:16**. [HIGH] — [Outfy Instagram Reel Size Guide](https://www.outfy.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/) (2026-07-31)
- Safe zone: keep key text/graphics out of **top ~250px** and **bottom ~350px** (center safe area ≈ 1010×1440); one source gives top 220px / bottom 450px as the conservative export margin. Also leave the **right ~10%** clear for the like/comment/share button rail. [MED] — [Outfy Safe Zone Guide](https://www.outfy.com/blog/instagram-safe-zone/), [Somake AI Reel Size Guide](https://www.somake.ai/blog/instagram-reel-size-guide) (2026-07-31)
- Export settings: MP4, H.264, 30fps, max file size 4GB. [MED] — [Fliki Instagram Reel Dimensions Guide](https://fliki.ai/blog/instagram-reel-dimensions-and-size-guide) (2026-07-31)
- **2026 change to watch**: Instagram's profile grid moved to a rectangular (~3:4) crop instead of square, so a cover/thumbnail designed edge-to-edge in full 9:16 can get trimmed on the grid — keep critical elements in the middle ~60%. [MED] — [Socialk Instagram Reel Size](https://socialk.it/en/sizes/instagram-reel-size), [Trustypost caption guide](https://trustypost.ai/blog/instagram-reel-caption-length-2026-best-practices-examples-that-get-watched/) (2026-07-31)

### Cover image
- Upload a dedicated 1080×1920px cover frame rather than relying on an auto-picked frame. [MED] — same sources as above (2026-07-31)

### Caption length
- Hard limit: **2,200 characters**. Only the **first ~125 characters** show before the "more" truncation in-feed — treat that first line as the real headline. [HIGH] — [Trustypost caption length guide](https://trustypost.ai/blog/instagram-reel-caption-length-2026-best-practices-examples-that-get-watched/) (2026-07-31)

### Hashtag guidance (official, from Adam Mosseri / Meta)
- Instagram now technically **limits posts/Reels to 5 hashtags**; Meta's own recommendation is **3–5 highly relevant tags**. [HIGH] — corroborated across multiple 2026 sources citing Mosseri directly (2026-07-31)
- Mosseri's stated philosophy: **"hashtags are not a way to get more reach"** — they're for categorization/searchability, not distribution. Over-tagging (>5) can read as low-intent to the algorithm and suppress reach. [HIGH] — [Facebook/Social Media Examiner recap of Mosseri statement](https://www.facebook.com/smexaminer/posts/adam-mosseri-says-instagram-is-limiting-hashtags-to-just-5-per-posthow-will-you-/1294179969418966/) (2026-07-31)
- What actually drives reach in 2026 per Mosseri: caption keywords, on-screen text, watch time, shares, saves. **Action item**: write captions with searchable plain-language keywords; treat hashtags as a minor tagging layer, not a growth lever.

### Trial Reels
- **What it is**: publish a Reel that's hidden from your own followers and shown only to non-followers via the Reels feed/Explore first; lives in a private "Drafts and trial reels" area. After ~72 hours, Instagram decides (based on non-follower engagement) whether to auto-share it to your followers/grid. [HIGH] — [Instagram for Creators official blog, "Trial reels"](https://creators.instagram.com/blog/instagram-trial-reels) (published 2024-12-10, accessed 2026-07-31)
- Engagement metrics become visible ~24 hours in; you can also manually force a share at any point.
- **Eligibility gotcha for a new account**: secondary sources consistently report Trial Reels require a **public account with ≥1,000 followers** — the official Instagram blog post does not itself state a number and instead defers to the Help Center. [MED/UNVERIFIED] — [Hiketop Trial Reels guide](https://hiketop.com/en/blog/instagram-trial-reels-explained), [PostFast Trial Reels guide](https://postfa.st/blog/instagram-trial-reels) (2026-07-31). **Implication for tangentideas.ai**: likely not usable until the account clears ~1K followers — don't build early strategy around it.
- Feb 2026 update: scheduling of Trial Reels in advance was added (batch a week of tests at once). [LOW/UNVERIFIED] — [Fliki Trial Reels guide](https://fliki.ai/blog/trial-reels-instagram) (2026-07-31)
- **Who should use it**: established creators (1K+ followers) testing new formats/niches without risking their main feed's coherence/aesthetic; not usable as a growth hack for brand-new accounts.

### Edits app (Meta's own editor)
- Records up to 10 minutes, shoots in 4K, and — critically — **exports without any watermark**. A beta feature extends export duration to ~15 min. Voiceover teleprompter added early 2026. [MED] — [Inrō "Meta Edits App 2026" guide](https://www.inro.social/blog/edits-new-meta-app), [Yahoo Tech coverage](https://tech.yahoo.com/social-media/articles/instagram-finally-lets-users-record-071000595.html) (2026-07-31)
- **Third-party editor penalty**: Instagram does **not** algorithmically penalize content merely for being edited outside Instagram/Edits. The actual penalty trigger is a **visible watermark** (e.g., from free CapCut/other exports) — watermarked content is ranked lower in the feed. Editing in Edits (or any watermark-free export) avoids this specific penalty; the "penalty for using third-party apps" as commonly framed is a myth — it's specifically a watermark/aggregator-content penalty. [MED] — [RouteNote "Does Instagram penalise TikTok or CapCut content?"](https://routenote.com/blog/does-instagram-penalise-tiktok-or-capcut-content/) (2026-07-31)
- Related 2026 policy: Instagram is cracking down on **content aggregator accounts** (reposting others' content); low-effort tweaks like watermark removal or speed changes don't count as "original," so an all-AI-generated channel should ensure content reads as genuinely original/transformed, not just reposted-and-tweaked. [MED] — same source (2026-07-31)

### Music/audio licensing: Business vs Creator account
- **Creator accounts**: full access to Instagram's trending/full music library. [HIGH] — [Napoleoncat Creator vs Business Guide](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business-account/) (2026-07-31)
- **Business accounts**: restricted to a much smaller commercially-cleared library (~14,000 tracks, the Meta Sound Collection); no trending/popular songs. This is a real algorithmic handicap — Business-account creators report materially lower Reels reach because they can't ride trending audio. [MED] — [TryMaas "Why Your Instagram Business Account Can't Use Trending Music"](https://www.trymaas.com/blog/instagram-business-account-trending-music-risks/) (2026-07-31)
- **Recommendation**: for a content-driven account like tangentideas.ai, run as a **Creator account**, not Business, specifically to retain trending-audio access (tradeoffs discussed in Section 6).

### AI-content labeling ("AI info" label)
- Meta added **"AI Info" labels** starting May 2024 on video/audio/image content across Facebook, Instagram, Threads when Meta **detects industry-standard AI indicators (C2PA metadata)** or when the creator **self-discloses**. [HIGH] — [Meta Transparency Center, "Labeling AI Content"](https://transparency.meta.com/governance/tracking-impact/labeling-ai-content/) (page reflects data through Oct 2024; accessed 2026-07-31)
- Meta itself states its detection "methodology is still evolving" and **may miss content edited with AI** that lacks embedded provenance metadata — i.e., detection is not comprehensive. [HIGH] — same source.
- **2026 update (ads-specific, most current)**: Meta expanded mandatory "AI info" labeling for **ads** in Ads Manager — using Background Generation, Image Generation, or Add Animation (or third-party tools like Photoshop generative fill/DALL-E/Canva AI detected via C2PA) auto-triggers the label; undisclosed AI use is now an active ad-rejection reason. Threshold is "significant modification" — minor edits don't require it. **This ads-specific mandatory rule is the clearest hard rule found; equivalent organic-post enforcement language for 2026 was not found in official sources** — treat organic posting as still governed by the 2024 "detect-or-self-disclose" framework above. [MED] — [CommonThreadCo "Meta's Mandatory AI Ad Labels"](https://commonthreadco.com/blogs/coachs-corner/meta-ai-ad-labels-mandatory-disclosure-ecommerce-2026), [Coinis "How Meta Labels AI Content"](https://coinis.com/blog/meta-ai-content-labeling-facebook-instagram-ads-2026) (2026-07-31)
- No official penalty schedule for non-disclosure on **organic** content was found (unlike YouTube, see Section 2) — this is [UNVERIFIED]. Given Meta is rolling out automatic detection, the safe operating assumption is: **self-disclose AI-generated/AI-voiced Reels via the built-in AI label option** rather than risk an automatic flag with unknown downstream consequences.

## 2. YOUTUBE SHORTS SPECS (2026)

### Length & aspect ratio
- Max length: **3 minutes** (raised from 60s in October 2024). [HIGH] — [Argil "How Long Can YouTube Shorts Be in 2026"](https://www.argil.ai/blog/how-long-can-youtube-shorts-be) (2026-07-31)
- Aspect ratio: **9:16 vertical**, same 1080×1920 canvas as Reels. [HIGH] — [ShortSync Upload Requirements 2026](https://www.shortsync.app/resources/youtube-shorts-upload-requirements-2026) (2026-07-31)

### Title/description best practice
- No official new hard limits found beyond YouTube's long-standing 100-char title / 5,000-char description limits (unchanged, not specific to Shorts). [LOW/UNVERIFIED — not separately re-confirmed in this research pass]

### Related-video link feature
- Lets a Short surface/link to a related **long-form video, livestream, or another Short** on your channel, set from YouTube Studio (desktop) → Content, after the Short is uploaded. The linked video must be public/unlisted and Guidelines-compliant. Purpose: convert Shorts traffic into long-form/channel growth. [HIGH] — [Official YouTube Help: "Add a related video to your YouTube Shorts"](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14075157?hl=en) (2026-07-31)
- **2026 UI change**: on 2026-06-26 YouTube moved the "Related Shorts" video placement to under the on-screen text and removed the literal "Related Video" label from the UI — viewers now just see channel/title/song. Also, since Aug 31 (year not fully specified in source — likely 2025), Shorts lost the ability to have clickable links inserted into comments. [MED] — [PPC Land "YouTube kills Clips and bets on timestamp sharing in 2026"](https://ppc.land/youtube-kills-clips-and-bets-on-timestamp-sharing-in-2026/) (2026-07-31)
- **Action item**: this related-video feature is the primary in-platform lever to push Shorts viewers into long-form content — should be part of the standard publishing checklist for every Short once a corresponding long-form video exists.

### Shorts audio licensing
- YouTube's in-app **Shorts audio library** contains label-licensed tracks, but licensing is **Shorts-specific and only for videos under 60 seconds** — using that same trending audio in a Short between 60s–3min (or in long-form/livestreams) can trigger a Content ID claim. [MED] — [Soundstripe "How to Use Copyrighted Music on YouTube Shorts"](https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/how-to-use-copyrighted-music-on-youtube-shorts), [PostLinkApp "YouTube Shorts Music Copyright Policy 2026"](https://postlinkapp.com/blog/youtube-shorts-music-copyright) (2026-07-31)
- Any Content ID claim on a Short over 60s risks a **global block**, not just demonetization. [MED] — same sources.
- AI-based music detection in 2026 can flag pitch-shifted/remixed/background-buried copyrighted audio. [LOW/UNVERIFIED — vendor blog claim]
- **Safe options**: YouTube Audio Library (free), YouTube Creator Music (licensed/revenue-share), or royalty-free libraries (Epidemic Sound, Artlist, Soundstripe). **Recommendation**: given this is an automated/API-upload pipeline (see Section 3), bake in only Audio-Library-cleared or fully-owned/AI-generated audio — do not rely on the in-app trending picker since API uploads can't select it live anyway.

### AI-content ("altered or synthetic") disclosure rules
- Official policy (introduced March 2024, still current per 2026 secondary coverage): creators must disclose when content is **"realistic" and could be mistaken for a real person/place/event** — e.g., digitally replacing a face, synthesizing a voice, making a real place/event appear to do something it didn't. [HIGH] — [Official YouTube Blog: "How we're helping creators disclose altered or synthetic content"](https://blog.youtube/news-and-events/disclosing-ai-generated-content/) (published 2024-03-18, accessed 2026-07-31)
- **Exempt**: production-assistance AI (scripts, ideas, captions, SEO/titles/tags), clearly unrealistic/animated content, minor edits (color, lighting, beauty filters), standard special effects (blur, vintage look). [HIGH] — same source.
- **How to disclose**: in upload flow, "Altered content" section → select Yes → YouTube adds a **"Modified or Synthetic" label** to the description. Voluntary disclosure does **not** hurt reach, search ranking, recommendations, or monetization per YouTube's own statement. [HIGH] — same source + [Influencer Marketing Hub AI Disclosure Rules](https://influencermarketinghub.com/ai-disclosure-rules/) (2026-07-31)
- **Enforcement in 2026**: YouTube stated it would eventually add enforcement for creators who "consistently choose not to disclose." As of **May 2026**, YouTube rolled out **automatic AI detection** that can apply the label even without creator disclosure — especially for content that could mislead (health, news, elections, finance get more prominent label placement). [MED] — [Windows Forum recap](https://windowsforum.com/threads/youtube-ai-disclosure-rules-label-realistic-deepfakes-in-2026.439959/), [MiniMatters "Official 2026 Guide"](https://minimatters.com/youtube-altered-or-synthetic-content-disclosure/) (2026-07-31)
- **Action item**: for tangentideas.ai's AI-generated content, self-disclosing "Altered/Synthetic" is low-risk (no reach penalty per official YouTube statement) and hedges against the newer auto-detection flagging it involuntarily with unknown framing. Content that is clearly stylized/animated (not photorealistic-human) does not need the label.

### Monetization thresholds (YouTube Partner Program, 2026)
- **Standard tier** (full ad revenue + all features): **1,000 subscribers** + either **4,000 public watch hours in the trailing 12 months** OR **10 million public Shorts views in the trailing 90 days**. [HIGH] — [vidIQ YPP Guide 2026](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-partner-program-guide/), [Nexlev YPP Requirements 2026](https://www.nexlev.io/youtube-partner-program-requirements) (2026-07-31)
- **Expanded/early tier** (Fan Funding only — Super Thanks, channel memberships, etc., not full ad revenue): **500 subscribers** + (**3,000 watch hours** OR **3 million Shorts views in 90 days**). [HIGH] — same sources.
- Shorts watch-time does **not** count toward the 4,000-hour long-form bucket — the two thresholds are alternate paths, not additive. [HIGH] — same sources.
- Also required: 2-step verification enabled, no active Community Guidelines strikes. Approval review typically takes 1–4 weeks after thresholds are met. [MED] — same sources.
- **Strategic note for tangentideas.ai**: because Shorts-views and long-form watch-hours are separate qualifying tracks, a Shorts-heavy channel can reach monetization via the 3M/90-day (early tier) or 10M/90-day (standard tier) Shorts-view path without needing traditional watch-hours at all — relevant to the channel's Shorts + long-form dual strategy.

## 3. PUBLISHING AUTOMATION FOR A SOLO OPERATOR

### 3a. Instagram Content Publishing API (Graph API)
- **Requirements**: register as a Meta developer, create an app in Meta App Dashboard, Instagram account must be **Business or Creator (professional)**. For **Standard Access** (publishing only to your own account — this operator's case), **no App Review or Business Verification is required**; those are only mandatory for **Advanced Access** (managing other people's accounts). Permission needed: `instagram_business_content_publish` (Business Login for Instagram) or `instagram_content_publish` (Facebook Login for Business). [HIGH] — [Meta for Developers: Instagram Platform Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview/) (accessed 2026-07-31)
- **Rate limit**: officially calculated as **4,800 × number of impressions on the account in the trailing 24h** (Instagram Business Use Case rate limiting) — for a low-follower new account this can functionally cap you at a modest handful of publishes/day early on, loosening as the account grows impressions. Secondary sources report varying flat caps in practice (25, 50, or 100 posts/24h, with Reels/Stories sharing the same bucket) — treat those flat numbers as directional, not authoritative, given they conflict with each other. [HIGH for the formula / MED for the flat-number folk-numbers] — official doc above; secondary conflicting figures from [Netrows Instagram Graph API Guide 2026](https://www.netrows.com/blog/instagram-graph-api-guide-2026), [InstantDM Rate Limits Guide](https://instantdm.com/blog/instagram-api-rate-limits-explained-2026-developer-guide) (2026-07-31)
- **Can it publish Reels with cover + audio?** Yes for **cover** (a `cover_url` or `thumb_offset` param on the REELS media container — if both are set, `cover_url` wins). **No for Instagram's native music library** — API-published Reels cannot attach trending/catalog IG audio; audio must already be baked into the uploaded video file. There is a narrow audio-search/attach capability, but only for accounts connected via **Facebook Login for Business**, not plain Instagram Login. [HIGH for cover, MED for audio limitation] — [Meta Developer Docs: Content Publishing](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/content-publishing/), corroborated by [RapidDev Reels API automation guide](https://www.rapidevelopers.com/api-automations/how-to-automate-instagram-reels-scheduling-using-the-api), [Statusbrew "Add Trending Audio to Scheduled Reel"](https://statusbrew.com/insights/adding-trending-audio-to-scheduled-content) (2026-07-31)
- **Implication**: for a fully automated pipeline, audio strategy must be "burn audio into the render" (music-library tracks, licensed beds, or voiceover/AI narration mixed into the exported MP4) rather than relying on selecting IG trending sounds at publish time.

### 3b. Meta Business Suite native scheduling
- Free, works on web + mobile, no third-party app needed. Scheduling window: **20 minutes to 29 days ahead**; up to **25 posts/day, 75 days in advance** per one source (note: the "75 days in advance" and "29 days ahead" figures are inconsistent between sources — likely reflects different features, e.g., Stories vs feed/Reels — treat cautiously). [MED] — [SocialBee "How to schedule Instagram Reels in 2026"](https://socialbee.com/blog/schedule-reels-on-instagram/) (2026-07-31)
- Reel length cap noted for Business Suite scheduling specifically: 90 seconds (older/native-scheduler-specific limit, doesn't reflect the newer 3-min Reels cap). [LOW/UNVERIFIED — may be stale info in source] — same source.
- Requires Business or Creator (professional) account; Business Suite is itself the underlying mechanism through which the Graph API operates for many workflows.

### 3c. Third-party schedulers (Buffer / Later / Metricool)
- All three are **Meta Business Partners** using the official Graph API (not scraping/browser automation) for Instagram; Buffer and Metricool also support **YouTube Shorts** upload. [MED] — [Hashtag Tools "Buffer vs Later vs Metricool 2026"](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/buffer-vs-later-vs-metricool-best-scheduler-2026/) (2026-07-31)
- **Buffer**: free plan = 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts/channel, no time limit; supports Instagram + YouTube Shorts direct auto-publish. [MED] — [HowSociable Buffer Review 2026](https://howsociable.com/reviews/buffer) (2026-07-31)
- **Later**: no permanent free tier in 2026 — 14-day trial only; strongest for Instagram-first visual grid planning. [MED] — [Hashtag Tools comparison](https://hashtagtools.io/blog/buffer-vs-later-vs-metricool-best-scheduler-2026/) (2026-07-31)
- **Metricool**: free tier = 1 brand, up to 50 posts/month + analytics + competitor tracking; supports Reels scheduling via official API, but users report issues with custom YouTube Shorts thumbnails. [MED] — [Efficient App Metricool Review 2026](https://efficient.app/apps/metricool) (2026-07-31)
- None of these tools can inject native Instagram trending-audio selection either (same API limitation as 3a) — audio still must be pre-baked into the file.

### Recommended path — Instagram
For a solo operator wanting maximum automation with minimum ongoing cost: **use the Instagram Graph API directly (Standard Access, no App Review needed since you only manage your own account) for programmatic publish, with audio pre-mixed into renders**, and a scheduler (Buffer's free tier, or Metricool's free 50/month tier) as the human-friendly calendar/queue layer on top rather than hand-rolling a scheduler. This avoids Business Suite's more opaque scheduling-window quirks while staying fully within Meta's compliant, partner-approved automation path. [Synthesis — MED confidence, based on above HIGH/MED findings]

### 3d. YouTube Data API v3 upload
- **Quota model**: each Google Cloud project gets **10,000 units/day** by default (resets midnight Pacific). Historically `videos.insert` cost **~1,600 units**; Google reduced this to **~100 units per call as of Dec 4, 2025**. As of a **June 1, 2026** change, video uploads now draw from their **own dedicated daily bucket of ~100 calls**, separate from the shared 10,000-unit pool used for search/reads — meaning uploads no longer compete with analytics/read calls for quota. [MED — recent 2025/2026 policy changes reported by developer-tooling blogs, not independently cross-checked against an official Google changelog in this pass] — [GetPhyllo "YouTube API Quota Limits 2026"](https://www.getphyllo.com/post/youtube-api-limits-how-to-calculate-api-usage-cost-and-fix-exceeded-api-quota) (2026-07-31)
- **Net effect if accurate**: a default (non-extended-quota) project could upload roughly **~100 videos/day** rather than the old ~6/day ceiling. Given this is a load-bearing operational figure, **verify directly against the current Google Cloud Console quota page for your own project before relying on it** — this figure could not be corroborated against an official Google source in this research pass and should be flagged [UNVERIFIED] until confirmed live.
- **"Unlisted until verified" / private-lock gotcha**: any video uploaded via `videos.insert` from an **unverified API project created after 2020-07-28** is automatically locked to **private**, regardless of the privacy status you requested (including "unlisted") — public visibility requires passing a Google **API Audit** for that project. There's no per-video appeal; the fix is either complete the audit or re-upload manually via YouTube Studio/app. [HIGH] — official mechanism described consistently in [Google's own Help Center: "Videos locked as private"](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7300965?hl=en) and corroborated by developer reports (2026-07-31)
- **Action item**: before building the automated YouTube upload pipeline, complete Google's **API Audit / verification process** for the project up front — otherwise every API-uploaded Short will silently sit private and never reach the public feed, which would silently break the whole publishing pipeline.

### Recommended path — YouTube
Use the **YouTube Data API v3 directly** for uploads (simplest, first-party, well-documented), but treat **API Audit completion as a hard prerequisite**, not a nice-to-have — do this before any automation goes live. Buffer (free tier) can serve as a secondary human-facing scheduling/queue UI if desired, but the core upload path should be direct API to avoid adding a dependency/cost layer for a task the first-party API already does natively.

## 4. ANALYTICS ACCESS

### 4a. Instagram Insights API (professional accounts)
- **Media-level (official, documented)**: `impressions`, `reach`, `engagement` (likes+comments combined), plus (per secondary sources) **saves**, **shares/reposts**, and **video views**. Official Meta docs explicitly did not enumerate a full metrics list in the overview page and defer to endpoint-specific reference docs. [MED — official overview page confirms core metrics but is not exhaustive] — [Meta for Developers: Instagram Insights](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/insights/) (accessed 2026-07-31)
- **2026 metric changes**: Meta **deprecated** `Media impressions`, `Reel plays`, `Reel replays`, `Reel initial plays` — consolidating them into a unified **"Views"** metric. **Added** (~April 2026): `Profile reposts`, `Reposts` (post/media level), and **`Reels skip rate`** (closest available proxy to a retention/drop-off signal). [MED] — [socialsamosa / Elfsight Instagram Graph API 2026 guide](https://elfsight.com/blog/instagram-graph-api-complete-developer-guide-for-2026/) (2026-07-31)
- **No true retention curve / per-second watch data** and **no explicit "average watch time" metric** were found in available documentation for Reels via the API — `Reels skip rate` is the nearest substitute for retention, not a full curve. [MED/UNVERIFIED — absence of evidence, not confirmed absence]
- **Account-level**: impressions, reach, profile views; aggregated views/likes/comments across placements (Instagram + cross-posted Facebook + boosted media) as of 2026. [MED] — same sources.
- **Data retention**: the in-app Insights dashboard only shows the **trailing 90 days**; the underlying API/data-connector layer reportedly retains **up to 2 years** (or since account conversion to professional). **Practical implication**: if you want historical trend data beyond 90 days, you must pull and store it yourself via API on an ongoing basis — don't rely on the app to retroactively show old data. [MED] — [Minter.io "How to access historical insights data for Instagram"](https://minter.io/blog/how-to-access-historical-insights-data-for-instagram/) (2026-07-31)

### 4b. YouTube Analytics API
- Confirmed available metrics (official Google docs): `estimatedMinutesWatched`, `averageViewDuration`, `averageViewPercentage`, `views`, `engagedViews`, and retention-adjacent metrics `audienceWatchRatio`, `relativeRetentionPerformance`, plus segment-level `startedWatching`/`stoppedWatching`/`totalSegmentImpressions`. [HIGH] — [Google Developers: YouTube Analytics Metrics reference](https://developers.google.com/youtube/analytics/metrics) (accessed 2026-07-31)
- **No Shorts-specific "swiped away"/viewed-vs-swiped metric is documented in the official API reference** — that data is exposed in the **YouTube Studio UI** (Shorts-specific engagement panel) but not confirmed as a distinct, separately-named API field. Retention is only available as **segment buckets** (`elapsedVideoTimeRatio` dimension), not a true continuous per-second curve matching the Studio graph. [HIGH for API gap, MED for UI-only availability] — same official source (2026-07-31)
- **Practical conclusion**: swipe-away/completion-rate analysis for Shorts is best done by **reading `averageViewPercentage` + segment retention via API** as a proxy, but for the actual "viewed vs swiped in first 3 seconds" style number, **pull it manually from YouTube Studio's Shorts analytics UI** since it isn't a confirmed discrete API field.

### 4c. Practical export paths when APIs are gated/insufficient
- **Meta Business Suite**: has a native "Ads Reporting"/Insights export to **CSV/PNG/PDF** (All Tools → Advertise → Ads Reporting → export as CSV). Useful to merge with GA4/CRM/Shopify data for a fuller picture without building API integration. ### Instagram's stance: engagement bait vs keyword CTA with real deliverables

## 6. ACCOUNT SETUP HYGIENE

### Creator vs Business account tradeoffs (new account)
- Both are "professional" accounts (vs. Personal) with analytics, ads, and API access; free, and switchable anytime without losing followers. [HIGH] — [Napoleoncat Creator vs Business Guide 2026](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business-account/) (2026-07-31)
- **Music library**: Creator = full/trending library; Business = restricted ~14,000-track commercially-cleared library only. This is the single biggest practical tradeoff for a content-first account. [HIGH] — Section 1 sources above.
- **Creator-only features**: full music library, Creator Marketplace, Subscriptions, Live Badges. **Business-only features**: Shopping, physical address display, phone/booking contact buttons. [MED] — [Napoleoncat guide](https://napoleoncat.com/blog/instagram-creator-vs-business-account/) (2026-07-31)
- **API access**: conflicting secondary claims exist online (some say Creator accounts lack API access entirely), but the **official Meta developer documentation confirms both Business and Creator professional accounts are supported** for Graph API content publishing and DM automation — the real dividing line is Personal vs. Professional, not Creator vs. Business. [HIGH, resolving the conflict via primary source] — [Meta for Developers: Instagram Platform Overview](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/overview/) (2026-07-31)
- **Recommendation for tangentideas.ai**: **Creator account** — it retains trending-audio access (important for reach) while losing nothing needed for API publishing or DM automation, since both require only "professional account" status, not specifically Business.

### Linking IG to a Facebook Page — current requirement status
- A Facebook Page connection is **not strictly mandatory** to have a professional (Business/Creator) account, but **Insights, ad tools, and the full professional/API feature set require it in practice** — without a linked Page you lose access to Instagram Insights and ad tools. No official 2026 policy change was found removing this linkage requirement. [MED] — [Meta Business Help Center: Connect/disconnect Instagram and Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/connect-instagram-to-page) (2026-07-31)
- **Recommendation**: link a (even minimal/unused) Facebook Page at setup time to avoid losing Insights/API access later — this is a one-time low-cost setup step with no found downside.

### New-account "warmup" — lore vs evidence
- **Shadowbanning itself is real and Meta-acknowledged** — reduced recommendation/visibility for policy violations is a documented mechanism, not folklore. [MED] — [Kofluence "Shadowban Instagram In 2026: Real or Myth?"](https://www.kofluence.com/blog/is-instagram-shadowban-real-or-myth/) (2026-07-31)
- **However, the specific "7–14/10–21 day warmup schedule" numbers circulating in creator/marketing blogs are not sourced to any official Meta statement** — these figures come exclusively from SEO/growth-marketing blogs repeating each other, not from Meta engineering or help-center documentation. **Tag: [LOW/UNVERIFIED]** for any specific day-count warmup claim. — [Multilogin "How to Warm Up Social Media Accounts 2026"](https://multilogin.com/blog/how-to-warm-up-accounts/), [360uniquizer "Instagram Account Warmup 2026"](https://360uniquizer.com/en/news/instagram-account-warmup-2026) (2026-07-31)
- **What does appear credible and Meta-acknowledged-adjacent**: Mosseri/Meta engineering have stated the 2026 discovery engine is AI-recommendation-driven, not hashtag-driven — i.e., "hashtag tricks" as a warmup lever are explicitly debunked by Instagram leadership itself. - **Most credible real triggers for restriction** (as opposed to warmup folklore): (1) using third-party engagement-automation tools (auto-likes/auto-follows/auto-comments) is the most consistently documented real trigger for genuine restriction; (2) posting identical/near-identical clips from multiple accounts gets fingerprint-detected and flags all accounts using that clip. [MED] — [360uniquizer Shadowban guide](https://360uniquizer.com/en/news/shadowban-tiktok-instagram-2026) (2026-07-31)
- **Recommendation**: don't over-invest in a rigid manual "warmup ritual" (unsupported by primary sources); do avoid the two well-corroborated real risk factors — third-party engagement-automation tools (distinct from the Meta-API-compliant publishing/DM tools discussed above, which are fine) and posting duplicate content across multiple accounts.
