- **Beat map** (via cross-post transcript, `[OBSERVED via transcript, cross-post proxy]`): hook (name the

- **Open loop:** "hundreds more secret codes" opened at the very start, closed only by the off-platform DM

  a DM action).
- **Pacing:** rapid-fire numbered delivery, no dead air described in the transcript, consistent with high
  cut/change frequency (qualitative call per §3.3.2, not independently frame-verified).
- **Loop design (rewatch bait):** weak — the content is a reference list a viewer would more likely
  *screenshot or save* than rewatch start-to-end, which is itself a strong save-trigger per §2.7.

### 4. Value proposition
- **Practical value class:** copyable workflow / tool discovery — concrete, named, immediately usable
  prompt-prefixes, not vague gesturing.
- **Specificity:** high — exact code words given (TRUTHMODE, ELI10, etc.) with a one-line mechanism each,
  not just "use better prompts."
- **Walk-away value:** a viewer can literally copy 8-10 named strings and use them in their next ChatGPT
  session with zero further research — maximal immediacy.

### 5. Psychology scoring (against 03_virality_science.md)
- **STEPPS:** **Social Currency** (HIGH — "secret," insider-knowledge framing; sharing/using these codes

  arousal spike on its own); **Public** (LOW — no visible trending sound/challenge mechanic); **Story**
  (LOW — pure list, no narrative arc).
- **Primary emotion + arousal:** mild awe / practical-curiosity, moderate arousal — not a high-arousal
  anger/anxiety video (contrast with Video 2 below).
- **Identity-signaling share motive:** high — forwarding "10 secret ChatGPT codes" to a friend/group chat
  signals the sharer is ahead of the curve on AI, a clean fit for §2.5's identity-signaling mechanism.
- **Save/send trigger:** very strong — this is close to the platonic ideal of "list content" that
  `03_virality_science.md` §2.7 flags as disproportionately saved (though the specific "list content

### 6. CTA & funnel mechanics
- **Explicit CTA:** "Follow and comment 'CODES'" — comment-keyword gate, `[OBSERVED]`. This is the
  single highest-comment-rate video in this dossier's 7-video sample (3.46%), directly attributable to
  the gate (comments are structurally required to receive the payoff, not organic reaction).
- **Funnel position:** pure top-of-funnel viral bait — no Blotato mention, no link, nothing beyond the
  IG-native follow/DM loop (see Section A funnel map).

### 7. Replicability verdict
- **Extractable template:** (1) pick a tool everyone already uses (ChatGPT); (2) frame a known feature
  (prompt-prefixes/system-style instructions) as "secret codes," a reframe that manufactures novelty out
  of commodity information; (3) deliver a numbered list of 8-10 with one-line mechanism each, no filler;
  (4) gate the "extra" ones behind a comment/DM keyword; (5) keep the caption's first line as the CTA,
  not a teaser.
- **Commodity vs. unfair advantage:** the underlying technique (prompt-prefix instructions) is commodity
  knowledge, freely available elsewhere — Sabrina's unfair advantage is packaging/naming (memorable
  labels like "TRUTHMODE," "HORMOZI") and an existing large follower base to seed the early-velocity
  window (§1.1.3).
- **Difficulty for Tangent:** LOW-MED — the format itself (numbered listicle + comment gate) is fully
  reproducible with zero unique access; the hard part is landing on labels/hooks catchy enough to clear
  the "why would I share this" bar, which is a copywriting skill, not a resource constraint.

### 8. Why it won
- The single most load-bearing factor is **topic-class fit**: "secret codes for a tool you already use
  daily" combines high Social Currency, high Practical Value, and a near-zero comprehension barrier —
  every viewer already has ChatGPT open. Secondary factor: the comment-gate CTA converted watch-time into
  an unusually high **comment rate (3.46%)**, and per `03_virality_science.md` §1.1.1-1.1.2, comments and
  reshare-likelihood are among Instagram's most heavily-weighted ranking signals, plausibly compounding
  initial reach into the "audition" expansion wave described in §1.1.3.
- **Crucial caveat, established later in this dossier (Section C):** the *near-identical* repeat of this
  exact format 3 days later (Video 4 below) earned only 267K views — 16x less. This single fact means
  topic-class + CTA-format alone are demonstrably **insufficient** to explain the 4.2M outcome; some
  combination of first-mover novelty and early-velocity-window luck must also be doing real work (full
  argument in Section C).

## C. Boom-Bust Decomposition (centerpiece analysis)

**The core question:** what makes Sabrina Ramonov's 4.2M and 3.3M hits categorically different from her
23.8K median (a 176x spread — the largest raw top/median ratio directly measured in this 24-account
study)? This dossier's own cross-platform research (not just the IG harvest) makes this the
best-evidenced boom-bust case in the study, because **5 of the 7 analyzed videos have a dated,
independently-verified cross-post on YouTube and/or TikTok** — giving genuine natural-experiment leverage
that most single-platform IG dossiers in this study lack.

### Finding 1: Topic class is necessary but nowhere near sufficient — the 16x same-topic gap proves it
Video 1 (4.2M, "secret codes," 2026-05-24) and Video 4 (267K, near-identical "secret codes" content,
2026-05-27, caption text independently re-confirmed as effectively identical by this dossier) are the
same idea, same account, 3 days apart — yet differ by **16x**. This single comparison, established
directly from primary data in this dossier (Section B, Video 4), is the strongest evidence available that
**topic selection alone cannot explain the mega-hit tier.** `[OBSERVED]`, HIGH confidence for the raw
numbers and caption-identity; the causal explanation for the 16x gap remains `[INFERRED]` (see Video 4,
Section 8 — Instagram's stated near-duplicate/"already posted" penalty per §1.3.1 is the best-grounded
candidate, alongside plain early-velocity-window variance per §1.1.3).

### Finding 2: The mega-hit tier is IG-specific — the SAME content caps out 100-1000x lower on YouTube/TikTok
This is the dossier's most novel, directly-observed finding, made possible by the same-day cross-post
matches this dossier independently located and dated:
- The **3.3M** IG hit (Video 2, "ERASE," 2026-07-02) has a **same-day** YouTube cross-post
  (`MRxoP0-XIUY`) that earned only **31,655 views** — **~104x lower**, on the exact same day, with
  near-identical script content. `[OBSERVED]`, HIGH confidence (both dates independently confirmed via
  direct metadata pulls).
- Both **flops** (Videos 6 and 7, 7,575 and 10,168 views respectively) have same-day cross-posts on BOTH
  YouTube (3,207 and 1,785 views) AND TikTok (5,075 and 3,325 views) — `[OBSERVED]`, HIGH, all four dates
  independently confirmed to 2026-07-24.
- **Pattern:** across every single dated comparison this dossier could construct, Instagram is the only
  platform where this creator's content reaches a mega-hit tier (millions of views); the identical or
  near-identical content on YouTube Shorts and TikTok never exceeds ~80,000 views in either of this
  dossier's own 60-video (YouTube) and 20-video (TikTok) samples. The account's entire boom-bust
  character — the reason it was flagged for this study at all — is **structurally an Instagram-algorithm
  phenomenon**, not a property of the content itself (since the content is, per the cross-posts, largely
  interchangeable across platforms). `[INFERRED]` synthesis from the `[OBSERVED]` cross-platform numbers
  above — a reasonable, well-supported read of the pattern, though this dossier cannot rule out
  platform-specific audience-composition differences (e.g., her YouTube/TikTok followings may simply be
  smaller/less-primed subcultures than her IG following) as a contributing confound alongside pure
  algorithm differences.

### Finding 3: The comment-gate CTA reliably manufactures its target signal, but doesn't guarantee reach
Every one of the 7 analyzed videos except Video 5 (personal reflection) and Video 6 (flop) uses a
"follow + comment/DM [KEYWORD]" gate. Comment rates for CTA-gated videos in this sample range 1.38-3.50%;
the one flop lacking the CTA (Video 6) has a comment rate of just 0.053% — a **~40-65x gap attributable to
the mechanic itself**, independent of view count (Video 7, the CTA-gated flop, still hit 3.50% comment
rate despite only 10.2K views — the highest comment rate in the entire sample, mega-hits included).
`[OBSERVED]`, computed directly from harvest + this dossier's independently re-confirmed like/comment
counts, HIGH confidence for the correlation; `[INFERRED]` for causation (a comment-gate CTA is the most
parsimonious explanation for the pattern, consistent with `03_virality_science.md` §3.4.3's documented
mechanism, but this dossier did not run a controlled test). **The practical implication: the CTA mechanic
is a reliable engagement-signal generator, not a reach generator** — it cannot be credited with the
mega-hit outcomes on its own, since it's present on both mega-hits AND on Video 4's 267K near-repeat AND
on Video 7's 10.2K flop.

### Finding 4: Emotional arousal class is directionally consistent with, but not sufficient to fully
explain, the winner/flop split
Video 2 (3.3M, privacy/identity anxiety — high-arousal per §2.3) and Video 6 (7,575 views, deepfake-scam
anxiety — theoretically also high-arousal) are structurally very similar (both anxiety-framed, both

**~440x**. This is a genuine complication for a clean "emotional arousal explains virality" story:
`[OBSERVED]` topic/emotion similarity, `[OBSERVED]` massive outcome gap. The two lowest-arousal videos in

end of the sample (272K top-5-but-weakest-engagement, and a flop, respectively) — directionally

predictor. `[INFERRED]` synthesis.

### Verdict: ranked contributing factors (this dossier's honest best synthesis, not a single silver bullet)
1. **Platform-specific algorithmic lottery in the early-velocity window (§1.1.3) — the single largest,
   least controllable factor.** The 16x same-topic/same-account/3-days-apart gap (Finding 1) and the
   100x+ same-day/cross-platform gap (Finding 2) both point to a genuinely stochastic distribution
   mechanism as the dominant driver of WHICH specific posts become the 2/1000+ mega-hits, more than any
   controllable content property this dossier could isolate. **This is the honest, appropriately-hedged
   headline finding: for this creator, mega-virality is best modeled as "post good content in a proven
   topic class repeatedly and often, and occasionally Instagram's algorithm rolls a jackpot" — not as a
   deterministic formula she (or Tangent) could reliably re-trigger on demand.**
2. **Topic-class selection is a real but partial lever.** Both mega-hits and the strongest near-misses
   cluster in exactly two franchises — "secret/insider ChatGPT techniques" and "AI-agent-mode
   personal-stakes demos (privacy/identity)" — while the two flops sit in comparatively lower-arousal or
   less personally-urgent territory (generic scam-awareness, trip logistics). This is directionally
   useful (topic selection is a real, controllable input) but Finding 1's 16x same-topic gap proves it is
   nowhere near sufficient on its own.
3. **The comment-gate CTA is necessary-adjacent but not sufficient** — present on both hits, but also on
   a 267K near-miss and a 10.2K flop; it reliably boosts engagement-conditional-on-reach (Finding 3) but
   cannot be shown to drive reach itself.
4. **Novelty/first-mover timing plausibly matters** — Video 2's "agent mode" demo landed on a newly
   prominent AI capability; Video 1 was the FIRST "secret codes" post, with its near-repeat (Video 4)
   earning 16x less — both consistent with a first-mover/novelty-decay pattern, though this dossier
   cannot rule out the "already posted" algorithmic penalty (§1.3.1) as the more mechanical explanation
   for the same observation.

## Compact Summary (for downstream synthesis)

**Variance-cause verdict:** The 176x top/median gap is best explained as **platform-specific algorithmic
lottery within a self-reinforced, high-volume topic-franchise strategy** — not a repeatable creative
formula. The strongest direct evidence: (1) near-identical content posted 3 days apart on the SAME
account differed 16x (4.2M vs. 267K); (2) content cross-posted same-day to YouTube/TikTok never exceeded
~80K views on those platforms even when it hit 3.3M on Instagram. Topic class (two recurring franchises:
"secret/insider ChatGPT techniques" and "AI-agent-mode personal-privacy demos") and the comment-gate CTA
are real, controllable, already-systematically-exploited levers — but both are demonstrably present on
her worst flops too, so neither is sufficient on its own. The honest takeaway for Tangent: repeated
high-volume attempts within 1-2 proven high-arousal topic classes raises the odds of a jackpot; it does
not manufacture one on demand.

**Narrative-vs-reality notes:** Her "3M+ followers / 41.9M views per 30 days" marketing claim (used to
sell Blotato) is not fabricated — it sums real, mostly-verifiable numbers across ~9 platforms and ~250
posts/week — but it materially outpaces what any single post on her most-scrutinized platform (Instagram,
this study's harvest target) typically delivers: a 23.8K median reel against 1M followers (2.38%), the
20th-ranked engagement efficiency of 22 ranked accounts in this study. TikTok and YouTube medians (≈1.5%
and ≈2.5-4.1% of followers respectively, independently pulled by this dossier) are comparably modest. A
Blotato customer replicating the "250 posts/week, cross-post everywhere" mechanic without also landing 1-2
rare organic outliers would likely see results closer to these observed medians than to the aggregate
headline figure. Independently, Blotato itself carries a 2.0/5 Trustpilot rating (84% one-star, MED
confidence, not independently re-opened due to a 403 block) — a real, if secondhand, signal of gap
between the polished growth-system narrative and the product experience it's sold on top of.

**Formula bullets (what's actually replicable):**
- Reframe commodity knowledge (prompt-prefixes everyone could look up) as "secret/insider codes" —
  packaging, not novelty, creates the Social Currency.
- Attach genuinely current AI capabilities (agent mode) to personally-stakes-relevant demos (privacy,
  identity) rather than abstract productivity tips — highest-arousal, highest-performing topic class
  observed.
- Gate the exact copy-pasteable payoff behind a comment/DM keyword — reliably inflates comment volume
  (a heavily-weighted ranking signal per official IG documentation) regardless of reach, at effectively
  zero cost.
- Post the SAME proven topic franchise repeatedly (5-9+ attempts observed per franchise, across
  platforms) rather than chasing one-off ideas — raises the odds of catching an early-velocity-window
  jackpot, per this dossier's Finding 1-2.
- Reserve occasional personality/vulnerability posts (Video 5) for after an audience already has an
  established relationship with the account's usual content — this dossier found no evidence this format
  works as a cold-start strategy.

**Surprises:**
- The clearest, most useful finding didn't come from the IG harvest data alone — it came from
  independently locating **same-day cross-posts on YouTube and TikTok**, which turned a "possible one-day
  dip" hedge in the original harvest file into a confirmed three-platform pattern, and turned the account's
  boom-bust character from an unexplained IG anomaly into direct evidence that the mega-hit tier is
  Instagram-algorithm-specific, not a property of the content itself.
- A single caption (Video 5) contains the creator's own on-record admission that she personally dislikes
  the exact DM-automation mechanic her top 6 other analyzed videos all rely on — rare, genuinely
  first-person evidence (not analyst inference) that the funnel mechanic is a deliberate, self-aware
  tactical choice rather than an incidental habit.
- The comment-gate CTA's effect is cleanly separable into two independent questions this dossier could
  actually distinguish with data: it reliably manufactures comments (works on Video 7's flop just as
  well as on the mega-hits) but has no demonstrable relationship to reach itself (absent on Video 6's
  flop, present on Video 4's 267K near-miss) — a more precise, mechanism-level finding than "CTAs help.".
