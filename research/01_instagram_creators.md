## Methodology notes & caveats

- No Instagram login was used. All follower counts come from search-engine snippets of third-party sources (HypeAuditor, Socialveins, Socialpruf, Favikon, press coverage, self-reported creator claims) — never scraped directly from Instagram's live UI.
- **Avg reel views were almost never independently discoverable** for individual creators via search — Instagram does not publish this publicly, and paid tools (HypeAuditor/Modash full reports) sit behind paywalls that blocked direct fetch (e.g., HypeAuditor returned HTTP 403 on direct fetch). Where noted, "estimates" use published aggregate benchmarks (e.g., "accounts >50K followers average ~65,800 views/reel" — Dash Social 2026 benchmark) as a rough, explicitly LOW-confidence proxy — these are NOT account-specific measurements and should be re-verified with a paid tool (Modash/HypeAuditor full report, or direct Instagram Insights access) before using in outreach decisions.
- Several creators have **multiple/fragmented Instagram accounts** under similar names (Ankit Arora: @ai_ankitarora vs @aiwithankit; Kallaway: @kallaway vs @kallawaytech; Sebastien Jefferies: @sebastienjefferies vs @sebastien) — flagged inline; verify the correct primary handle before any outreach.
- The "niche fit" judgment call (YES / BORDERLINE) separates creators whose **primary content pillar is AI tools/education/automation** from creators who are broader business/marketing/tech generalists that touch AI as one theme among several (Dan Martell, Chris Donnelly, Sorav Jain, Sundas Khalid). Both groups can be useful depending on campaign goals, but they are not interchangeable.
- Coverage swept: (a) 2025-2026 "top AI influencer" roundups [Favikon, Apify/100-influencers-to-follow, Kolsquare, listnr.ai, rightmedia.ae, pixelagency.ai], (b) tool-specific searches [n8n, Claude Code, Midjourney, CapCut, Adobe Premiere/Photoshop, Sora/Runway], (c) AI money/side-hustle searches, (d) faceless/meme/news-explainer AI pages, (e) non-US creators [India — Vaibhav Sisinty, Varun Mayya, 100xEngineers, Ankit Arora, Sorav Jain; Latin America/Spain — mostly surfaced virtual personas rather than human AI-education creators, a genuine gap worth a follow-up sweep in Portuguese/Spanish specifically for human creators].
- **Gap flagged for follow-up**: the Adobe-editing-workflow and pure-Midjourney-artist subniches did not surface any individual human creator clearing even the 200K bar — the accounts found (@midjourney.gallery 222K, @midjourneyartwork 157K) read as fan/gallery/curation pages rather than personal creator brands, and were excluded from the main table for that reason. A deeper look at YouTube-to-Instagram crossover editors (people who post Adobe/CapCut AI workflow tutorials) is recommended as a distinct follow-up pass.
