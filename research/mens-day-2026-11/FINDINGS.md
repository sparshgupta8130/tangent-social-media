## 3. What IMD content got praised and what backfired

- **Backfired: Gillette, "The Best Men Can Be", Jan 2019.**
  - What it was: a #MeToo-framed lecture film.
  - Reaction: within days, far more dislikes than likes, and boycott talk.
  - Sources: NPR, 2019-01-17. [HIGH]
  - Pattern: lecturing men about "toxic masculinity" drew the largest documented pile-on.
- **Praised in trade press (no numbers): emotional-permission content.**
  - MensXP #DontManUp with Bhuvan Bam, Nov 2021: a poem against "men don't cry". [Social Samosa, MED]
  - The Man Company #GentlemanInYou, 2021. [MED]
- **2025 India trade roundup: "Brands embrace emotional strength over toughness".**
  - Hunter beer: a film on male friendship ("Men don't need words, they just get it").
  - MediBuddy: #PowerUpGuys, on physical and mental health.
  - Source: exchange4media, Nov 2025. [MED]
- **Indian IMD comment culture:**
  - Memes about the day being ignored.
  - "Why no Google doodle?" bias claims.
  - Recurring gender-war replies.
  - Sources: Republic and Free Press Journal, 2020; Feminism in India, 2018. [MED]
  - Any post that sounds like a grievance invites this.
- **"Men Will Be Men" is a brand tagline, not a free meme.**
  - It is Imperial Blue whisky's tagline: first ad about 2000, still running in 2026.
  - Sources: ThePrint and exchange4media. [MED]
  - It is an alcohol brand's line. **Do not borrow it.**
- **"Men will literally do X instead of going to therapy":** a Twitter snowclone from 2020.
  - Source: Know Your Meme. [MED]
  - Commentary says it can shame men for not getting help. Sources: Mel Magazine 2021; AOL. [LOW-MED]
- **UK ASA gender-stereotype rule, 2019:**
  - It banned ads whose joke is a man being incompetent (e.g. Philadelphia's hapless dads).
  - Source: The Drum, 2019-08-14. [HIGH-MED]
  - Our inference: the safe joke is a man's affection or quirks, never his uselessness.

## 4. What makes people share

- **Berger & Milkman (2012), JMR 49(2):**
  - High-arousal emotions (awe, amusement, anxiety, anger) increase sharing.
  - Sadness, a low-arousal emotion, is linked to *less* sharing.
  - Positive content spreads more than negative.
  - [HIGH; the sample was news articles, not Reels]
- **Instagram:** Mosseri names watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach as the top Reels
  signals. Sends matter more for reaching non-followers.
  - Source: Social Media Today, reporting Mosseri, about Jan 2025. [MED]
  - The often-quoted "sends are worth 3-5x likes" has no primary source. Do not use it.
- **Humour as a way in to men's distress:**
  - JED Foundation x Clemson (7.2M online mentions): young men use humour and memes to signal
    distress. [MED]
  - A 2026 JMIR Infodemiology study found humour recurs as a coping theme. [MED]
- **Implication:** a purely sad post is the weakest bet for sharing. A post that is funny and
  recognisable ("that's my dad") with a kind last beat fits the evidence best.

## 5. AI content: what gets views and what gets praise

- **Big trends, with reach and reaction:**
  - Ghibli images, Mar 2025: stylised, not photoreal. Hugely popular, plus an ethics backlash. [TechCrunch, MED]
  - Nano Banana saree / retro-Bollywood / figurine portraits, Sep 2025, India-led.
    - Why it worked: the viewer's *own face* in a culturally resonant look.
    - Concerns raised: privacy flags. [Business Today, MED]
  - Veo 3 Bigfoot vlogs, May 2025: an absurd character vlog. A racist offshoot drew condemnation. [Media Matters, WIRED, MED]
  - Fake "Ring cam" bunnies, Jul 2025: about 183M-203M views, earned by fooling people. [PetaPixel, Lead Stories, MED-HIGH]
- **Praise is not the same as views.**
  - The bunny clip's reach came from deception, the opposite of disclosure-first.
  - Coca-Cola's 2024/25 and McDonald's NL's Dec 2025 AI Christmas ads drew "soulless/creepy"
    backlash. McDonald's pulled its ad after about 3 days. [NBC News, TechRadar, HIGH]
  - The criticism was about cold tone and execution, not AI alone.
- **Craft and human authorship help.**
  - The Kalshi Veo 3 NBA Finals ad (Jun 2025): a named director, 300-400 generations, 15 clips used. [NPR, MED-HIGH]
- **Indian audiences check cultural detail.**
  - JioHotstar's AI "Mahabharat" (Oct 2025) was mocked for anachronisms ("IKEA us time bhi raha hoga"). [Business Today, MED]
- **Audiences want to know what is AI.**
  - 76% of US adults say it is very or extremely important to tell AI from human-made work. [Pew, 2025-09-17, HIGH]
- **Platforms now label AI.**
  - YouTube auto-labels significant photorealistic AI use, more prominently since May 2026. [Tubefilter, MED]
  - Instagram limits reach for unlabelled AI-persona accounts from 31 Aug 2026. [Digital Trends, MED]
  - India's IT Rules amendment (G.S.R. 120(E), notified 10 Feb 2026, effective 20 Feb 2026)
    requires labelling of synthetic content "likely to be perceived as real". [Medianama, MED-HIGH]
- **Tools available now:**
  - Veo 3.1 / Flow: Ingredients-to-Video with reference images, Frames-to-Video, Extend, native audio. [Google blog, 2025-10-15, HIGH]
  - Nano Banana Pro (Gemini 3 Pro Image): up to 14 input images, up to 5 people kept consistent. [Google blog, Nov 2025, HIGH on the vendor claim]
  - Kling 3.0: multi-shot, native audio. [MED-LOW]
  - Runway Gen-4.5, Aleph 2.0 and Act-Two. [MED]
  - Sora app closed 26 Apr 2026; API closed 24 Sep 2026. [The Decoder, Engadget, HIGH]

## 6. The Jensen Huang faceswap wave

- **The premise is real.**
  - Nvidia's SEC filing of 12 Nov 2026: Huang resigned as CEO and from the board. [sec.gov, HIGH]
  - Press link the resignation to affair allegations, including an *alleged* tie to Chinese
    intelligence, which he denies. [CBS, Variety, CNBC, 2026-11-12, MED-HIGH]
  - AI parodies (a Matrix spoof, a kiss-cam spoof, a 50 Cent remix) have spread since about
    10 Nov. [Seoul Economic Daily, SBS, MED]
- **Part of the flood is state-linked disinformation.**
  - NewsGuard reports China-affiliated deepfakes: a fake interview with his daughter, a cloned
    voice of a family member, and a fake LinkedIn page.
  - Source: NewsGuard, Washington Times, 2026-11-17. [MED]
- **Indian law:**
  - The IT Rules 2026 labelling requirement (§5 above).
  - Delhi HC personality-rights orders through 2026 against AI deepfakes: Varun Dhawan, Ravi
    Kishan, Kriti Sanon (29 Oct 2026) and others.
  - The Sanon order reportedly carves out parody, but that carve-out is interim and untested. [MED]
  - Defamation (BNS s.356) is the realistic exposure when a "joke" implies false facts.
- **Consent tools leak.** Sora 2's opt-in "cameo" still produced unauthorised likenesses within
  weeks. [NBC News, Oct 2025, MED-HIGH]

## 7. LinkedIn and YouTube

- **LinkedIn ranking (2026):**
  - It is moving to LLM-based ranking.
  - It demotes engagement bait, generic AI-written posts and comment automation; such posts do
    not travel beyond direct connections.
  - Sources: LinkedIn news (2026) and Entrepreneur / TechRadar quoting LinkedIn's product VP. [MED]
- **LinkedIn dwell time:** a skip-prediction model penalises posts people scroll past.
  [LinkedIn Engineering, 2020, HIGH]
- **LinkedIn vulnerability posts:** they invite "LinkedIn Lunatics" cringe mockery when they read
  as performed. [The Star, Dallas News, 2024-10, MED]
  - An LSE study frames such posts as strategic self-branding. [MED]
  - No data on IMD posts on LinkedIn. [UNKNOWN]
- **YouTube Shorts:** ranked separately from long-form. [Search Engine Journal on a YouTube Q&A, MED]
  - No evidence that a festival or awareness day gives a search or trend bump. [UNKNOWN]
