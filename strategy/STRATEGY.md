(`research/`, `synthesis/01–03`). **What this is not:** a calendar, a fixed pillar list, or a
launch script. The planner derives schedules from §7's bands; the creative director derives
concepts from §4's selector. Routine weekly-review outcomes land in the bet register
(`BETS.md`); this document's own text changes only by Sparsh + Showrunner jointly, inside a
weekly review (`ops/ROLES.md` decision right (f)). The old `synthesis/04_TANGENT_STRATEGY.md` is superseded AS STRATEGY — it froze decisions
that belong to the team's judgment loop — but its concrete content ideas remain valuable:
they are mined, with provenance, into `strategy/CANDIDATES.md`, the candidate backlog that
`/plan campaign` and `/ideate` draw from.

Evidence convention: claims cite the corpus (`[CORPUS: file]`), the rubric
(`research/03_virality_science.md`), or demand research (`research/04`). Anything untested for
us specifically is a **bet** and lives in `BETS.md` with a validation gate.

## 2. Positioning: "Machine craft, human taste."

The story: **an engineer is teaching an AI taste** — his camera, his code, his taste. This is
a multi-year series, not a gimmick: photographs now; video, music, apps as later seasons.

- **Anti-slop, stated loudly and first.** Every documented backlash flashpoint (Cara exodus,
  SDCC/FanX bans, Meta's July-2026 Muse retreat) targeted *undisclosed*, *fully-autonomous*,
  or *other-people's-work* generation [research/04 §5]. We are structurally the opposite —
  own raws, multi-round critique, human gate — and the positioning must SAY so in the bio,
  in captions, and on-screen. Disclosure is a brand asset here, not a legal chore.
- **Sell the image, not the AI** (the AI-topic reach ceiling, [CORPUS: yt_skillleap]):
  pure-AI framing caps reach even when resonance is high. Hooks lead with the universally
  legible payoff — a breathtaking photograph, an impossible transformation, an agent doing
  something jaw-dropping — and the AI is the twist that lands after the scroll-stop.
- **Prove, don't assert** [CORPUS: 02_MEGA_VS_WANNABE §6]: live verifiable process is our
  credential. Real UI, real sliders, real critic rejections, real prints.

**Never-list (constitutional):** other people's images or IP; undisclosed generative
content; bought reach of any kind (three corpus autopsies: kallaway, handlytech, tiffintech
economics); bare engagement bait; hiding the AI; claiming autonomy the pipeline doesn't have.

## 3. The bets

Strategy = a portfolio of explicit bets with gates, not certainties. Live status: `BETS.md`.

- **BET-1 (lane):** AI + Lightroom/Photoshop fine-art editing is an underserved demand pocket
  we can own — zero human creators >200K in the subniche vs enormous demand signals
  [synthesis/03 WS-1].
- **BET-2 (lane):** "Claude for creatives" is a fast-growing, near-empty adjacency that
  compounds with BET-1 (r/ClaudeAI 250K→1M in 13 months; proven "comment CLAUDE" mechanics)
  [synthesis/03 WS-2].
- **BET-3 (format, the differentiation play):** demo-in-motion transfers from tool-demos to
  ART — "watch an agent edit like a master" is filmable only by us. Explicitly UNTESTED on
  art in the corpus; this is the highest-variance bet and carries a real validation gate
  [synthesis/03 WS-3; research/04 §3c calls it "a bet, not a demonstrated opportunity"].
- **BET-4 (backstop lane):** original-aesthetic art spectacle wins in LONG-FORM — the
  corpus-proven art genre lane [CORPUS: yt_curiousrefuge] — with all-rights-owned work
  replacing the IP-mashup treadmill. If BET-3 fails its gate, effort shifts here.
- **LENS-1 (always-on):** the 35+/calm-craft/jargon-free tone — a targeting lens on
  everything, not a separate lane. Creative-AI usage is gender-balanced but confidence is
  not; tone is the gap [research/04 §2].

## 4. Content architecture

**Tiers** (portfolio slots, not franchises — [CORPUS: 02 §5]):
- **HERO** — engineered for reach; the awe demos. Expensive, rationed, never rushed.
- **WORKHORSE** — identity and cadence: series episodes, workflow shares with gated
  deliverables. Sustains the median.
- **FILLER** — news-response within 0–4 days of Adobe/Anthropic/AI-imaging events,
  demonstrated on our own raws, never a talking-head recap. Cadence insurance, priced as
  such: filler protects hero quality, never replaces it.

**The concept selector** — a concept must pass ALL FIVE or die at ideation
(evidence: topic selection dominates format execution, [CORPUS: yt_nateherk 180x same-template gap]):
1. Universally relatable premise, or a universal frame wrapped around the niche topic.
2. A concrete, verifiable payoff shown on screen (not claimed — shown).
3. High-arousal target emotion, named in the brief; awe is the default for us.
4. Fits an owned format, or deliberately audition-tests a new one (max one audition in
   flight at a time).
5. We can prove it live: our raws, our pipeline, visible process.

**Format ownership:** converge on ≤3 owned formats; the median is plannable only when
formats repeat [CORPUS: ig_tiffintech, ig_elevenpercentprod]. Starting portfolio (subject to
kill/replace by weekly review, never mid-week): (a) the "Claude edits" demo-in-motion reel;
(b) a numbered cold-start series wrapper (series = follow-conversion, not view compounding
[CORPUS: ig_harpercarrollai]); (c) the workflow-share with keyword-DM deliverable.

Craft rules (hooks, loops, pacing, demo grammar) live in `knowledge/craft/` — this document
does not duplicate them.

## 6. Measurement constitution

1. **Plan on medians; treat spikes as windfalls** [CORPUS: yt_berman]. Growth accounting runs
   weekly on medians per tier.
2. **No single-sourced numbers.** Every stat in a postmortem passes the like-rate sanity band
   (1–10% organic) and a second-source check (`tools/verify_stats.py`) — the corpus's own
   instrument was wrong 15.5% of the time [synthesis/01 Law 0.1].
3. **Every hit AND flop gets the views×LR decomposition** — hook/discovery failure vs craft
   failure demand different fixes [synthesis/01 Law 0.2].
4. **Efficiency over followers:** median views ÷ followers is the health metric; small-account
   healthy band 10–15%, excellent 20%+ (Devin's 156% is an existence proof, not a benchmark).
5. **Funnel bar:** comment:like ≥ 0.5 on funnel posts = mechanic working; < 0.2 after 5
   posts = change the deliverable class before abandoning the mechanic.
6. **The audience-mismatch early-warning triad** (check monthly, [CORPUS: ig_handlytech]):
   efficiency sliding under ~5%; hits coming only from off-pillar content; flat non-pinned
   spread. Two of three → planner triggers a strategy review.
7. **Off-pillar windfall discipline:** enjoy it, capture it with a wrapper if possible, never
   rebuild the channel around it.

## 7. Cadence bands (planner picks within; never outside without a bet-gate decision)

- **Steady state:** 3–5 reels/week + 1 long-form/month. Floor 2/week.
- **Launch surge (time-boxed ≤30 days, at most once per quarter):** up to daily series
  episodes + 2 HERO/week — only while pipeline health holds (no G2 failures backing up,
  no stale BLOCKED-ON-SPARSH items older than 3 days).
- Honest ops model: solo + agents ≈ Riley's 4–5 crafted + trend posts [CORPUS: 02 §7].
  Staffed-cadence envy is banned; quality collapse shows up in the medians within weeks.

## 8. The moat (why this wins if executed)

A real, working, FILMABLE agentic art pipeline; a finished gallery as standing proof; the
engineering credibility to own the Claude lane; full rights to every pixel shown; and a
story arc — "an engineer taught an AI taste" — with years of seasons in it. Nobody in the
18-dossier corpus can copy the pipeline, and the audience can verify every frame of it.
