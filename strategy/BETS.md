# BETS — Live Register

The strategy's falsifiable claims, each with a validation gate and a kill/pivot trigger.
Owned by the weekly review (`/weekly-review`): status changes happen there and only there,
with the evidence written down. A bet is never "believed" — it is UNTESTED, VALIDATING,
VALIDATED, or FAILED.

Statuses: `UNTESTED` → `VALIDATING` (data accruing) → `VALIDATED` / `FAILED` (gate decided).

---

## BET-1 — The editing lane
**Hypothesis:** AI + Lightroom/Photoshop fine-art editing content can own an underserved
demand pocket (zero human creators >200K in subniche vs massive demand signals).
**Evidence base:** synthesis/03 WS-1; research/04 §3a.
**Validation gate:** by post 20 in the lane: account median/follower efficiency ≥ 10% AND
lane posts' median like-rate in the healthy 1–10% band with ≥1 post breaking 3x account median.
**Kill/pivot trigger:** 20 lane posts with efficiency < 5% and no post over 2x median →
re-examine premise packaging (frame, not topic) before abandoning lane.
**Status:** UNTESTED (account not yet launched). Notes: —

## BET-3 — Agentic editing as spectacle (THE differentiation bet)
**Hypothesis:** demo-in-motion grammar transfers from tool-demos to ART: "watch an AI agent
edit like a master" stops the scroll for a general audience.
**Evidence base:** synthesis/03 WS-3 — explicitly proxy-based; research/04 §3c: "a bet, not
a demonstrated opportunity." Corpus never tested this format on art.
**Validation gate:** after ≥8 HERO demo reels: HERO median ≥ WORKHORSE (series) median AND
HERO median LR in band. That's the minimum bar for "the format carries the lane."
**Kill/pivot trigger:** HERO median < WORKHORSE median after 8 posts → the art audience
prefers outcomes to process: shift HERO effort to BET-4 (long-form spectacle), demote
process reels to before/after teasers of the long-form. Do not run the experiment past 12
posts — sunk-cost discipline.
**Status:** UNTESTED. Notes: —

## LENS-1 — 35+ calm-craft tone
**Hypothesis:** jargon-free, calm, craft-first tone unlocks the 35+ majority (YT) and the
confidence-gap audience without costing reach among the young.
**Evidence base:** research/04 §1 (35+ = 51.9% of YT ad-reachable), §2 (confidence/sentiment
gap).
**Validation gate:** qualitative until audience data exists: comment sentiment + follower
demographics once IG insights unlock (100+ followers). Revisit at 1K followers.
**Kill/pivot trigger:** none — this lens costs nothing; drop only if comment data shows the
tone reads as flat (low-arousal) rather than calm. The awe must survive the calm.
**Status:** UNTESTED. Notes: —
