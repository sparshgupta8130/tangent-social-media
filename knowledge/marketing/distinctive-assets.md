# Distinctive Assets — Tangent's Asset Register

**Owner:** brand-director.
**Load context:** read before scripting any recurring visual/verbal device; before any BTS
"public identity surface change" flag (bio, name, tagline, cover-grid style); at every G3
review (checklist below); at the quarterly brand audit (`ops/RUNBOOK.md` §9).

Distinctive assets are the ownable, recognizable identifiers that compound recognition
through repetition — the growth lever `brand-science.md` §2 rates strongest and least
contested. Framework: Romaniuk's fame × uniqueness grid — an asset counts as "distinctive"
only once it scores high on both how many people link it to us (fame) and how exclusively
it points to us and not a competitor (uniqueness); high-fame/low-uniqueness means it works
for the whole category, not for Tangent. [CANON: Romaniuk, *Building Distinctive Brand
Assets*, Oxford University Press, 2018] [VERIFIED-ONLINE:
https://global.oup.com/academic/product/building-distinctive-brand-assets-9780190311506,
2026-07-31].

**Status key:** `UNBUILT` (defined, not executed) / `EMERGING` (executed once, no
repetition data) / `ESTABLISHED` (repeated consistently, `[OWN-DATA]` recognition exists).
Every asset below is UNBUILT as of this build (2026-07-31, pre-launch; all bets UNTESTED
[REPO: strategy/BETS.md]). This register updates only through the postmortem/
weekly-review write path (`knowledge/INDEX.md` write-control) — this is the baseline, not a
forecast.

## The register

**1. The name/wordmark — Tangent / tangentideas.ai / tangent.gallery.** [REPO: STRATEGY.md
§1]. *Build:* spoken/shown identically across surfaces, never abbreviated or re-branded
per platform (`knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md` bio drafts already fix this). *Protect:* no
sub-brand names for series/formats — a series gets a number (`content-architecture.md`),
never a new name. *Measure:* fame = % of comments/DMs using "Tangent" unprompted vs.
generic description; uniqueness is near-guaranteed for the literal string, so the job here
is fame-building.

**2. "Machine craft, human taste" (the line).** [REPO: STRATEGY.md §2;
`knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md`]. *Build:* reuse the exact phrase, never a paraphrase — a verbal
asset loses compounding value the moment it's "freshened up." *Protect:* never let a
guest/sponsor brief substitute their own tagline for it (`failure-modes.md` mode 5 applied
to an asset, not a format). *Measure:* fame = the line quoted back unprompted; uniqueness
checked against the competitive frame (`positioning-doctrine.md`) at the quarterly audit.

**3. Gallery-white minimal aesthetic + Fraunces serif.** [REPO: `knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md`].
*Build:* every asset passes the never-use list (no meme-fonts/red-arrows/neon) before G2 —
already a lens-5 craft-gate item; here the aesthetic itself is the recognizable signal, not
just a quality bar. *Protect:* no trend-chasing visual templates even on an otherwise
on-brief format — consistency (mere-exposure, `brand-science.md` §6) outweighs any single
post's discovery boost. *Measure:* fame = recognizing a Tangent frame from an
unlabeled screenshot; uniqueness benchmarked against `pattern-library.md` once corpus
comparison data exists.

**4. The critic-as-character.** [REPO: `audience/positioning.md`; `knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md`;
`craft/hooks.md`]. The highest-leverage unbuilt asset — it serves disclosure doctrine, the
confidence-gap persona (`audience/personas.md` persona (b): the critic's visible
fallibility lowers the bar for her own attempts), and distinctive-asset fame simultaneously.
*Build:* identical on-screen treatment every time — same label, typography, beat position
(`tangent-voice.md`: reused identically once adopted). *Protect:* verdicts stay real —
actual G1/G2 rejections [REPO: SYSTEM_DESIGN.md §8] — never staged; a fabricated rejection
would be a disclosure violation, not just a brand risk. *Measure:* likely fastest asset to
reach high fame+uniqueness, since nothing in the competitive frame has an equivalent; track
comment mentions of "the critic" by name.

**5. The before/after snap.** [REPO: `content-architecture.md` format (a)]. *Build:* always
a hard cut/wipe, not a slow fade — the transition style is the asset, independent of any
one photo. *Protect:* never a pair from a non-Sparsh raw (never-list); never juice the
"before" to look worse than shot (violates "prove, don't assert"). *Measure:* fame =
viewers anticipating the reveal (comments like "wait for it"); uniqueness is moderate on
its own — before/afters are common in editing content generally — so it clears the
uniqueness bar only in combination with the critic-character and demo-in-motion grammar.

**6. The sliders-moving-by-themselves visual.** [REPO: `knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md`;
`production/adobe-playbook.md`]. The literal, filmable pipeline signature. *Build:* always
genuine screen capture, never mocked/recreated — the entire value is that it's real and
independently verifiable, i.e. the moat [REPO: STRATEGY.md §8]. *Protect:* never
speed-ramp or cut around it in a way that reads as staged — highest-risk asset for the
AI-skeptic persona (`audience/personas.md` persona (d)) if it ever looked manipulated.
*Measure:* Tangent's most defensibly unique asset — nobody in the corpus has this pipeline.
Fame is the only real variable to track; uniqueness starts near-ceiling and stays there as
long as the build rule holds.

**7. Sparsh's builder-voice.** [REPO: `knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md`; `audience/positioning.md`
engage-with-craft protocol]. *Build:* one voice across every surface — register shifts
(kinetic on IG, direct/personal on email) but attributes don't. *Protect:* never let a
ghostwritten/generic-creator-voice caption slip in under time pressure — one off-voice post
is a small mere-exposure cost, but *consistent* off-voice posting resets the compounding
effect entirely. *Measure:* hardest to quantify — proxy via comment-tone matching once
volume exists; qualitative Brand Office read at G3 in the meantime.

## The G3 asset-usage checklist (brand-director runs this at every G3)

1. Which registered assets does this item use, and did each appear in fixed form (not
   "freshened up")?
2. Any new recurring device introduced that isn't in this register? Flag as a candidate
   addition — a new device competes for the same fame-building budget as the existing 7, so
   adding one is a real trade-off, not a free add.
3. Was any asset diluted this item — inconsistent, mocked instead of real, or paired with an
   off-brief guest/sponsor treatment (`failure-modes.md` mode 2/5)?
4. Does the caption/on-screen text reuse verbatim phrasing for any verbal asset, or did it
   paraphrase? Paraphrase = FAIL this item, logged as a G3 note even under an ADVANCE verdict.
5. This checklist's answers feed `critiques/G3-brand.md` under the Brand-Equity Impact
   lens as evidence — not a separate document or parallel gate.
