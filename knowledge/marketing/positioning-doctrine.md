## The one-line-description test

**The test:** if a stranger described tangentideas.ai after watching 5 reels, would they
land on: *"the engineer teaching an AI taste — real photos, real craft"*? [REPO:
STRATEGY.md §2 — the canonical one-line story]. This is the positioning-integrity check the
brand-director runs at G3 and at any BTS-triggered consult, and it is a pass/fail test,
not a spectrum — a description that's directionally close but drops a load-bearing element
(e.g. "an AI that edits photos," missing the human-gate framing, or "a photography account
that also uses AI," missing the authorship claim) is a FAIL.

**Operational form:** for any item at BTS≥3 or at G3, the brand-director writes the one
sentence a cold viewer would plausibly use to describe the account after this item, and
checks it against the target line above. A framing drift (e.g. "this is cool AI tech," with
the craft/taste half missing) is a positioning-drift HOLD trigger per `SYSTEM_DESIGN.md`
§12.4.

## Category-entry-point map for Tangent

Restates `brand-science.md` §1 operationally — each entry situation mapped to the tier/
format that serves it (`content-architecture.md` tiers).

| Entry situation (what a stranger is thinking) | Tier/format that serves it | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "How do I edit this photo" | WORKHORSE — workflow-share + keyword deliverable (format c) | direct answer to the stated need; the funnel gates the real payoff |
| "AI did something insane" | HERO — Claude-edits demo-in-motion (format a) | the awe entry point; the pipeline's spectacle is the payoff |
| "I want my photos to look professional" | WORKHORSE/HERO — before/after-led demo reel | universal premise (`content-architecture.md` selector criterion 1); payoff is the finished plate |
| "Is this AI slop or actually good" | any tier, via disclosure framing | served by *how* every format discloses, not a dedicated format — same doctrine as persona (d) in `audience/personas.md` |
| "What's Claude actually capable of" | WORKHORSE — numbered series wrapper (format b) | Claude-for-creatives entry point, BET-2 territory |
| "[Adobe/Anthropic] just shipped something" | FILLER — news-response | 0–4-day window, `virality/laws.md` Law 8 |

**Rule:** a brief that can't name which entry-point situation it's capturing has already
failed selector criterion 1 (`content-architecture.md`) before it reaches this map. The
table exists so the Brand Office can audit *coverage*: are we camping on one or two entry
points and starving the others, or is the weekly slate spread across the map. The
brand-director checks coverage at weekly review, not per-item.

## Competitive frame

**Who we're mentally substitutable with (the corpus's seed set)** [REPO:
research/00_seed_accounts.md]:
- **Sebastien Jefferies** (558K TikTok confirmed; IG unverified — DATA GAP in the source
  research) — AI-tools-decoded educator; teaches AI avatar/faceless-brand tactics,
  monetizes via a paid Skool community. Substitutable on "AI creative tools"
  surface-level interest.
- **Nate Herk** (~870K YouTube, 274K IG) — the n8n/AI-automation educator; teaches building
  agents, monetizes via Skool + agency + book. Substitutable on "Claude/AI agents"
  surface-level interest (BET-2's adjacency).
- **Devin Huynh / Eleven Percent** (84K IG, 40.7K YT) — AI-powered video-editing tools;
  built AutoEdit (a Claude-powered Premiere Pro plugin), monetizes via SaaS + presets +
  affiliate. Closest surface-level neighbor: "Claude + editing" is literally his
  positioning too.

**What keeps us non-substitutable:** none of the three seed accounts point their pipeline
at *fine-art photography editing as the content itself* — Sebastien and Nate Herk teach
automation/agent-building broadly (photography is not their subject), and Devin Huynh's
AutoEdit is a video-editing *product* sold to other editors, not a demonstration of an
agent editing to gallery-grade taste as the content. [REPO: STRATEGY.md BET-1 — "zero human
creators >200K in the subniche."] The differentiation claim here clears the bar
`brand-science.md` §2 sets (provable, not asserted): none of the three seed accounts, nor
the wider 18-dossier corpus, has a finished public gallery (tangent.gallery) as standing
proof behind the content — a structural difference, not a rhetorical one.

**Where the substitutability risk is real:** all three seed accounts monetize via paid
communities/courses/SaaS — if Tangent's WORKHORSE workflow-share content reads as "another
AI-tools tutorial account," it collapses into the same mental slot as Sebastien/Nate
Herk/Devin, and mental-availability research (`brand-science.md` §1) says being in a
crowded, undifferentiated slot means the *biggest* account in that slot wins the recall
contest by default (double jeopardy, `brand-science.md` §3) — not us, at 0 followers. The
category-entry-point map above exists partly to keep the account's center of gravity on
"AI did something insane"/photography-craft entry points where the field is thin, not just
"AI tools explained," where it's crowded.

## Brand architecture doctrine

[REPO: STRATEGY.md §1, restated for architecture/naming rules]

**The four surfaces and their roles:** IG (tangentideas.ai) reaches → YT deepens → gallery
(tangent.gallery) proves → email owns. Each surface has exactly one job; content is placed
by which job it serves, not by convenience.

**Naming rule:** the account name/handle stays "tangentideas.ai" on IG and
"tangent.gallery" for the proof surface — never a third variant name for YouTube or email.
A recurring named device (the critic label, a series number) may exist within a surface but
never as a competing brand name (`distinctive-assets.md` asset 1 protect rule).

**Cross-linking rule:** the bio link on tangentideas.ai points to tangent.gallery (the
proof surface), never to a lead-magnet/funnel page — link priority follows "prove, don't
assert," not conversion-optimization instinct [REPO: `knowledge/brand/tangent-voice.md` — "keeping
the bio link pointed at proof, not a lead magnet"]. The DM-funnel deliverable is
comment-gated, not bio-linked. YouTube descriptions link to the gallery for depth-seekers
and to IG for the reels themselves ("full build on the channel" CTA runs the other
direction, IG→YT, per STRATEGY.md §5).

**What lives where:**
- **tangent.gallery** — finished work only, no feed-chasing, no process content; the
  brand's quality anchor [REPO: STRATEGY.md §1].
- **tangentideas.ai (IG)** — the show, reels-first, HERO/WORKHORSE/FILLER per
  `content-architecture.md`.
- **YouTube** — native re-cuts of process content as Shorts (never blind cross-posts, per
  STRATEGY.md §1) plus finished-art spectacle long-form (BET-4).
- **Email** — the only owned asset; grows from the DM funnel; voice is the most direct/
  personal of the four surfaces (`tangent-voice.md`).

**Drift rules between surfaces:** voice (calm/precise/dry-wit) stays constant across all
four; register shifts by surface (IG most kinetic, gallery most still, YT in between, email
most personal) — a caption written as formally as an email under-performs on IG; an email
written like an IG caption under-delivers on the "owned, personal" register email exists to
build. Any surface expansion (new platform, new named sub-brand) is a BTS "new platform
surface" flag (+1) at minimum and requires this file's naming/cross-linking rules to be
extended explicitly, never inferred ad hoc per-post.
