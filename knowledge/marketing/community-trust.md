## Parasocial-relationship ethics and mechanics

**Base research:** parasocial relationships are nonreciprocal socio-emotional bonds
viewers form with media figures — strengthened by perceived dialogue (comment replies,
direct camera address) and self-disclosure, and measurably increasing trust and downstream
behavior. [REPO: research/03_virality_science.md §2.4.1, citing MDPI *JTAER* 2025, a CUNY
graduate thesis, and an SSRN paper on parasocial trust].

**The ethical obligation this creates:** a parasocial bond is real trust from a real
person, even though nonreciprocal — the audience-advocate represents that person's interest
inside the Brand Office, since nobody else is structurally positioned to. Two duties follow: (1)
never exploit the bond's trust for something the audience wouldn't knowingly consent to
(undisclosed commercial framing, manufactured scarcity) — the trust-side reading of the
never-list in `audience/positioning.md`; (2) the bond compounds with Sparsh's consistent
on-screen/on-voice presence (`distinctive-assets.md` asset 7) — protecting that consistency
is a trust duty, not just a brand-asset one. **[EXTRAPOLATION]:** the research establishes
parasocial bonding as real and consequential; it doesn't itself prescribe these specific
conduct rules — they are the Brand Office's operationalization.

## Comment-section doctrine

**Extends** `audience/positioning.md`'s engage-with-craft protocol with community-building
specifics that file doesn't cover:

- **Who/when:** Sparsh (or content clearly in his voice, `distinctive-assets.md` asset 7)
  handles replies — no ghostwritten voice, ever, since a mismatched reply is itself a
  trust-ledger event. Target window: 24–48h while the account is small enough to make this
  achievable [**[EXTRAPOLATION]** — practitioner convention, no hard research sets an
  optimal window for a sub-10K account].
- **Craft-appreciation:** engage warmly and specifically, referencing the actual
  technique/decision — a specific reply compounds mere-exposure/parasocial bonding
  (`brand-science.md` §6) more than a generic "thank you."
- **AI-curiosity:** answer concretely per engage-with-craft step 1 (`positioning.md`) — also
  the highest-value comment type for surfacing FILLER/WORKHORSE brief candidates.
- **AI-hostility:** governed by the engage-with-craft protocol (`positioning.md` steps
  1–4); this file's addition is sequencing — one calm, concrete reply per genuine objection,
  then stop; don't let a hostile thread consume response effort disproportionate to its
  actual audience size, since persona (d) isn't a follower target to begin with.
- **Generic/low-effort:** no response obligation — comment volume is a positive signal
  (`benchmarks.md` funnel ratio) regardless of reply rate; never manufacture engagement-bait
  replies to inflate counts (violates THE LINE, `platform/instagram.md` §2).

## The trust ledger

A running, qualitative account of whether Tangent kept its word — formalize into a weekly
section once comment/DM volume justifies it. Three components:

1. **Disclosure consistency.** Every post matches or exceeds the disclosure bar in
   `audience/positioning.md`. A single perfunctory/buried disclosure is a ledger debit even
   with no visible backlash — the audience-advocate flags it at G3 regardless of
   consequences avoided, because the ledger tracks actual consistency.
2. **Promise-keeping.** The DM-funnel deliverable arrives instantly and is genuinely useful,
   every time (`platform/instagram.md` THE LINE; `publishing-ops.md` §4's
   live-test-before-shipping rule). A thin deliverable is the fastest way to burn trust with
   persona (c), the Claude-curious builder, who explicitly "distrusts gated content that
   turns out thin" (`audience/personas.md`) — every funnel post's pre-publish checklist
   gates on a live test for this reason.
3. **Error-owning.** Factual/technical/process errors are corrected publicly and visibly — a
   pinned comment or follow-up post, never a silent edit. Mirrors the corpus's own
   retraction discipline (`knowledge/INDEX.md`) applied to audience-facing content. Public
   correction reinforces the critic-as-character asset (`distinctive-assets.md` asset 4) —
   showing the fallible, self-correcting process is brand-reinforcing, not damaging, per
   disclosure-is-an-asset (`positioning.md`).

## Crisis protocol

**Classification first** — name the type before drafting any response:

| Type | Definition | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Craft dispute | quality/legitimacy of an edit disputed | Engage-with-craft protocol — show the specific human decision, invite scrutiny |
| Disclosure challenge | claims we under-disclosed AI involvement | Self-audit against `positioning.md`'s doctrine before any reply — correct first if merited, defend second, never the reverse |
| IP accusation | claims a shown asset isn't ours/infringes | Verify against the never-list immediately (constitutional, `CLAUDE.md`); escalate to Sparsh before any statement if the claim has any merit |
| Platform strike | IG/YT flags or restricts a post/account | Verify against `platform/instagram.md`'s penalty list and `publishing-ops.md`'s setup rules before assuming malice or error; document for the postmortem regardless |

**First-response templates** — hold the line, don't improvise under pressure:
- *Craft dispute:* "Fair to ask — here's exactly what happened: [specific brief/rejection/
  adjustment]. Happy to post the reject if useful." [REPO: `positioning.md`'s own verbatim
  example].
- *Disclosure challenge (we were right):* name the specific disclosure element with a
  timestamp/quote — don't argue the principle, point at the evidence.
- *Disclosure challenge (we were wrong):* "You're right, that wasn't clear enough — here's
  the correction: [specific fix]." Acknowledge before defending, every time.
- *IP accusation:* "This is [Sparsh]'s own photograph, shot [context if safe] — happy to
  share the raw file's metadata." Never a dismissive denial without verifiable evidence.
- *Platform strike:* no public statement until internal verification completes — a
  premature "we did nothing wrong" that's later wrong is worse than a delayed, accurate one.

**Who decides:** any public statement beyond the templated first responses requires Sparsh
in the loop — consistent with `SYSTEM_DESIGN.md`'s human-input protocol (public-identity/
reputation matters aren't autonomously resolved) even though a crisis reply isn't literally
SHIP/money/face. The audience-advocate drafts classification + recommended response; Sparsh
approves before it posts, in both modes — a `BLOCKED-ON-SPARSH` item with same-day urgency,
not standard due-date cadence.

## Community-building sequencing for a 0→10K account [EXTRAPOLATION]

No hard research sets an optimal sequencing for a solo creator brand by follower milestone
— the following is practitioner convention, explicitly tagged:

- **0–100 followers:** only 1:1 work is worth doing — reply to everyone personally; the
  disproportionate attention is the highest-leverage trust deposit available at this size.
  No dedicated rituals/polls/AMAs — premature per `brand-science.md` §5's CBBE framing
  (nothing above Salience exists yet to build rituals on).
- **100–1,000:** comment-reply discipline continues; the asset register
  (`distinctive-assets.md`) should start showing EMERGING status on the critic-character
  and sliders visual — recurring commenters recognizing the format is the earliest real
  community signal. Named-series framing (`content-architecture.md` format b) starts
  converting viewers to followers via commitment-signaling (Law 7, `virality/laws.md`).
- **1,000–10,000:** Trial Reels and real Insights unlock [REPO: `platform/instagram.md` §4;
  `strategy/BETS.md` LENS-1 — revisit at 1K followers]. A lightweight recurring community
  touch (regular Q&A thread) becomes worth auditioning as a single format slot
  (`content-architecture.md` audition rule: max 1 unproven format in flight).
  Monetization-readiness discussion begins in earnest only at 10K
  (`growth-playbook.md` monetization-readiness criteria) — trust-ledger health is one
  qualitative gate checked before that conversation, not after.
