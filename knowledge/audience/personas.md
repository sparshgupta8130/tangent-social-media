# Audience Personas

Load-bearing for G1/G2 lens 6 (AUDIENCE FIT & SHAREABILITY) and for brief-writing at
`/ideate`. Grounded in `research/04_whitespace_demographics.md`; every sizing number stays
honest about scope — we serve a small subset of a subset, not "everyone interested in AI."

## Sizing caveat (read first)

Only ~12% of US adults have ever used AI to create/edit an image or video (24% of the ~49% who
use AI chatbots at all) — the utility-AI audience (search, work tasks: 42–45%) is far larger
than the art-AI audience we serve [RESEARCH-BACKED: Pew 2026, research/04 §2]. This explains why
the corpus's biggest winners are tool-demo/automation creators, not art creators — they serve
the bigger pyramid tier. Frame every reach projection against this ceiling, not against "AI is
massive right now."

## (a) The 35+ craft-curious professional

**Platform:** YouTube-majority — 35+ is 51.9% of YT's ad-reachable audience vs. 35.4% of
Instagram's, and YouTube reaches a real majority at every age band, unlike Instagram's youth
concentration (65+ = only 19% reach) [RESEARCH-BACKED: research/04 §1].
**Wants:** a genuine craft process, not a trick; concrete technique they could apply or at
least understand; calm pacing, not youth-coded urgency.
**Distrusts:** hype vocabulary, jump-cut density for its own sake, anything that reads as "kids
selling me a course."
**Reached by:** WORKHORSE/HERO demo-in-motion on YT long-form or native Shorts; jargon-free,
calm-confident on-screen text (`craft/scripting-voice.md`); news-event hooks tied to Adobe/
Anthropic releases they'd plausibly have already read about elsewhere.
**Shares/saves:** saves technique they can reference later; sends it to a specific
photographer/colleague friend (STEPPS Triggers + Practical Value, research/03 §2.2, §2.7).

## (b) The creative-AI-curious with the confidence gap

**Platform-agnostic**, reached on both IG and YT.
**Demographics:** creative-AI usage is gender-balanced (24% men / 23% women), but confidence and
sentiment are not — "very confident" 22% (M) vs 15% (W); net sentiment +16 (M) vs −10 (W)
[RESEARCH-BACKED: Pew 2026, research/04 §2]. This is a tone gap, not a usage gap — she is
already doing the activity, just less sure of herself and less enthusiastic about AI as a
category.
**Wants:** permission to feel competent; a process that looks approachable, not a wizard's black
box.
**Distrusts:** "look how easy/instant this is" framing that implies she should already know it;
bravado.
**Reached by:** the critic's visible fallibility (rejections, revisions shown honestly) — proof
that even the "expert" system iterates, lowering the bar for her own attempts; calm-tone
WORKHORSE content, never HERO spectacle alone.
**Shares/saves:** saves workflow-share posts as reference; shares content that makes AI feel
learnable rather than intimidating (Social Currency via "I found something that actually
explains this," research/03 §2.2).

## (c) The Claude-curious builder/creative

**Platform:** IG-primary for discovery, funnels to owned list.
**Demographics/growth:** r/ClaudeAI grew 250K→1M subscribers in 13 months, accelerating; Claude
Code developer awareness rose 31%→57% in a year [MED-HIGH: research/04 §3e]. Proven mechanic:
"comment CLAUDE"-class keyword posts already produce outlier engagement in the corpus
(elevenpercentprod's rotating keyword funnel) [CORPUS: pattern-library §4.1].
**Wants:** the actual workflow — prompts, settings, the specific Claude-driving-Lightroom
mechanic — not a teaser.
**Distrusts:** gated content that turns out thin or non-functional once DM'd.
**Reached by:** the keyword-DM funnel (BET-2, `strategy/BETS.md`) gating a REAL deliverable
(process PDF, prompt, preset); WORKHORSE workflow-share format
(`knowledge/brand/content-architecture.md`).
**Shares/saves:** DMs the keyword (highest-intent action in the funnel); saves the post as a
reference before requesting; shares within builder communities (r/ClaudeAI-adjacent) where
being "plugged in" is social currency.

## (d) The AI-skeptic photographer

**Platform:** encountered mostly via comments/shares from others, not a primary follower — this
persona is reached, not necessarily converted to a follow.
**Context:** a real, mobilized population — Cara grew 40K→650K users in one week (June 2024)
fleeing undisclosed AI-training use; SDCC banned AI art from its 2026 show; FanX banned it from
its vendor floor (Sept 2025); ArtStation saw sustained protest [RESEARCH-BACKED: research/04
§5]. Every one of these flashpoints targeted **undisclosed, uncredited, or fully-autonomous**
generation, or use of other people's work without consent — never disclosed, human-curated,
tool-assisted craft.
**Wants:** proof of a human gate; proof the images are ours; to not be lectured or provoked.
**Distrusts:** "AI made this" bravado; anything reading as replacing photographers rather than
one photographer directing a tool.
**Reached by:** disclosed-craft framing ONLY (`audience/positioning.md`) — own raws stated
plainly, the critic shown as a real quality gate, never an "autonomous AI artist" claim. **This
audience is never provoked** — no content is built to bait or "own" AI skeptics; the engage-
with-craft protocol (`audience/positioning.md`) governs any comment-section contact.
**Shares/saves:** rarely shares proactively; if reached well, comments with grudging respect
("okay, that's actually a real process") — the realistic best outcome, not conversion to
follower.

## Persona → format/tier quick map

| Persona | Best-fit tier | Best-fit owned format | Never |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) 35+ craft-curious professional | HERO, WORKHORSE | (a) demo-in-motion reel on YT; long-form for spectacle | fast-cut youth-coded pacing with no craft payoff |
| (b) Confidence-gap creative | WORKHORSE | (c) workflow-share with visible critic iteration | "so easy a child could do it" framing |
| (c) Claude-curious builder | WORKHORSE | (c) keyword-deliverable workflow-share; (b) series wrapper | a gated deliverable that turns out thin |
| (d) AI-skeptic photographer | any, via framing only | none dedicated — reached through disclosure, not targeted content | any "autonomous AI artist" or undisclosed framing |

**Reading this table:** persona (d) has no owned format of its own by design — per
`strategy/STRATEGY.md` and `audience/positioning.md`, this audience is served by *how* every
format discloses its process, not by a piece of content built to address it directly. Building
content "for" AI skeptics risks reading as provocation; disclosed craft reaches them as a
byproduct of doing everything else honestly.
